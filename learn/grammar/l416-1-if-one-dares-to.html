JLPT N1Unit 16

～ようものなら

If one dares to ...

How to build it
V volitional + ものならV volitional + ものなら + bad result
What it means

V volitional + ものなら says that if something should happen, a serious result follows.

  • It imagines a risky action and its grave consequence.
  • The result is almost always negative.
  • Often with a boss or a strict person.
Think: 'if I should dare to ...'.
Forms
VerbForm
遅れる遅れようものなら
言う言おうものなら
Example sentences
  1. 遅刻ちこくしようものなら、部長ぶちょうに怒鳴どなられる。Chikoku shiyō mono nara, buchō ni donarareru.If I dare to be late, the manager will shout at me.
  2. そんなことを言いおうものなら、大変たいへんなことになる。Sonna koto o iō mono nara, taihen na koto ni naru.If you dare say such a thing, it will become a big issue.
  3. 秘密ひみつを漏もらそうものなら、ただでは済すまない。Himitsu o morasō mono nara, tada de wa sumanai.If you leak the secret, you won't get off lightly.
Common mistakes
  • 褒めようものなら、喜ぶ。 → 漏らそうものなら、怒られる。
    The result is a bad consequence.
