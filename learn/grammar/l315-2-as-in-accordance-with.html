JLPT N2Unit 15

～に従って

As; in accordance with

How to build it
V る + に従ってN + に従って
What it means

Use V る / N + に従って for a change that goes step by step, or for following a rule.

  • As A changes, B changes in step.
  • Both halves are changes that go together.
  • The second half can't be a plan or request.
  • With a rule or order, it also means 'in accordance with'.
The bigger A grows, the bigger B grows.
Two senses
JapaneseMeaning
年を取るに従ってas one gets older
指示に従ってin accordance with the instructions
Example sentences
  1. 高たかい山やまに登のぼるに従したがって、空気くうきが薄うすくなる。Takai yama ni noboru ni shitagatte, kūki ga usuku naru.The higher you climb, the thinner the air gets.
  2. 時間じかんが経たつに従したがって、痛いたみが弱よわくなった。Jikan ga tatsu ni shitagatte, itami ga yowaku natta.As time passed, the pain grew weaker.
  3. 年としを取とるに従したがって、体力たいりょくが落おちてきた。Toshi o toru ni shitagatte, tairyoku ga ochite kita.As I get older, my stamina has declined.
Common mistakes
  • 年を取るに従って、食べよう。 → 年を取るに従って、食欲が落ちる。
    Don't end in a plan or request.
