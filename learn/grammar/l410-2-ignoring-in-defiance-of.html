JLPT N1Unit 10

～をよそに

Ignoring ...; in defiance of

How to build it
N + をよそに
What it means

N + をよそに means doing something without caring about worries or objections.

  • The subject acts without caring about N.
  • N is a worry, criticism or expectation.
  • It is used in news and essays.
Remember: よそ = elsewhere, so the mind is elsewhere.
Typical nouns
NounMeaning
心配worry
反対opposition
Example sentences
  1. 彼かれは親おやの心配しんぱいをよそに、一人ひとりで旅たびに出でた。Kare wa oya no shinpai o yoso ni, hitori de tabi ni deta.Ignoring his parents' worries, he set off on a trip alone.
  2. 住民じゅうみんの反対はんたいをよそに、工事こうじが進すすめられた。Jūmin no hantai o yoso ni, kōji ga susumerareta.Ignoring residents' opposition, construction went ahead.
  3. 周囲しゅういの期待きたいをよそに、彼かれは引退いんたいした。Shūi no kitai o yoso ni, kare wa intai shita.Going against everyone's expectations, he retired.
Common mistakes
  • 親の愛をよそに、感謝した。 → 親の心配をよそに、遊んだ。
    N must be something the person ignores.
