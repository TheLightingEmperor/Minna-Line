～ついでに

While you're at it, on the way

How to build it
V る / V た + ついでにN の + ついでに
What it means

Use ～ついでに when you take advantage of doing one thing to do a second, smaller thing.

  • The first action is the main one.
  • The second action is an easy extra.
  • Nouns are action nouns, like 買い物 or 散歩.
  • ついでに can also start a sentence alone.
Use it alone when adding a request: ついでにパンも買ってきて。
Main job and extra job
Main actionExtra action
ゆうびん局へ行く切手を買う
散歩をするパンを買う
そうじをする窓をふく
Example sentences
  1. コンビニへ行いくついでに、ごみを出だしてきて。Konbini e iku tsuide ni, gomi o dashite kite.While you're going to the convenience store, take the rubbish out.
  2. 買かい物もののついでに、本屋ほんやに寄よった。Kaimono no tsuide ni, hon'ya ni yotta.While out shopping, I stopped by a bookshop.
  3. そうじをしたついでに、窓まどもふいた。Sōji o shita tsuide ni, mado mo fuita.While I was cleaning, I wiped the windows too.
Common mistakes
  • お茶を飲むついでに、新聞を読む。 → お茶を飲みながら、新聞を読む。
    Two things at the same moment take ながら.
