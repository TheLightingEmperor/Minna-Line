JLPT N1Unit 6

～ともなると

When it comes to ...

How to build it
N + ともなるとV + ともなると
What it means

N / V + ともなると means that when a situation reaches a certain level, things naturally change.

  • It describes what is natural at a certain status or time.
  • The result is a fact, not a wish.
  • It often has a feeling of 'of course'.
Use it when the topic is a rank, an age or a special time.
Compare
PatternFeel
ともなるとnatural result at a high level
ともなればsame, more emphatic
Example sentences
  1. 大学生だいがくせいともなると、親おやに頼たよらず生活せいかつする人ひとも多おおい。Daigakusei to mo naru to, oya ni tayorazu seikatsu suru hito mo ōi.Once you are a university student, many people live without relying on their parents.
  2. 年末ねんまつともなると、街まちは人ひとであふれる。Nenmatsu to mo naru to, machi wa hito de afureru.When it comes to the year's end, the town overflows with people.
  3. 社長しゃちょうともなると、責任せきにんは重おもい。Shachō to mo naru to, sekinin wa omoi.When you become president, the responsibility is heavy.
Common mistakes
  • 教師ともなると、行きましょう。 → 教師ともなると、責任が重い。
    The second half is a fact, not an invitation.
