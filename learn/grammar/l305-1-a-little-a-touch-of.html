JLPT N2Unit 5

～気味

A little; a touch of

How to build it
V ます-stem + 気味N + 気味
What it means

Use V ます-stem / N + 気味 for a slight sign or feeling, often a bad one.

  • It means a slight sign of something.
  • The thing is usually unwelcome.
  • Common words are 風邪気味, 疲れ気味 and 緊張気味.
  • It is not used for strong or serious states.
Add 気味 when you want to say 'a touch of'.
Examples
WordMeaning
風邪気味a slight cold
太り気味a little overweight
寝不足気味a bit short on sleep
Example sentences
  1. 風邪かぜ気味ぎみなので、今日きょうは早はやく帰かえります。Kaze gimi na node, kyō wa hayaku kaerimasu.I have a bit of a cold, so I'm going home early today.
  2. 最近さいきん、少すこし太ふとり気味ぎみだ。Saikin, sukoshi futori gimi da.Lately I'm gaining a little weight.
  3. 仕事しごとが多おおくて、疲つかれ気味ぎみです。Shigoto ga ōkute, tsukare gimi desu.I have so much work that I'm a bit worn out.
Common mistakes
  • ひどい風邪気味です。 → 少し風邪気味です。
    気味 means slight, so don't pair it with strong words.
