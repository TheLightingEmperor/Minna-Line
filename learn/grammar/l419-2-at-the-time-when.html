JLPT N1Unit 19

～ところを

At the time when ...

How to build it
V / adj + ところをお + adj + ところを
What it means

Verb / adj + ところを is a polite opener: 'in spite of the situation you are in'. Also used for a sudden event.

  • It acknowledges the other person's situation.
  • It follows with thanks, apology or request.
  • It can also mark an event interrupted in action.
A polite phrase for emails: お忙しいところを恐れ入ります.
Typical lines
LineMeaning
お忙しいところをwhen you are busy
お休みのところをwhile you rest
Example sentences
  1. お忙いそがしいところを、恐おそれ入いります。Oisogashii tokoro o, osore irimasu.Sorry to trouble you when you are so busy.
  2. 寝ねているところを、電話でんわで起おこされた。Nete iru tokoro o, denwa de okosareta.I was woken by a call just as I was sleeping.
  3. 帰かえろうとしたところを、呼よび止とめられた。Kaerō to shita tokoro o, yobitomerareta.Just as I was about to leave, I was stopped.
