JLPT N2Unit 7

～に沿って

Along; in line with

How to build it
N + に沿ってN + に沿った + N
What it means

Use N + に沿って for following a rule, plan or guideline, or for going along a line.

  • It means going along a line, or following a rule or plan.
  • It suits guidelines, manuals, policies and roads.
  • Use に沿った + noun before a noun.
  • The 'line' can be real or an idea.
Stay on the line, whether it is a river or a rule.
Two senses
JapaneseMeaning
道に沿って歩くwalk along the road
指示に沿って動くact in line with the instructions
Example sentences
  1. マニュアルに沿そって、作業さぎょうを進すすめてください。Manyuaru ni sotte, sagyō o susumete kudasai.Please carry out the work following the manual.
  2. 川かわに沿そって、桜さくらの木きが並ならんでいる。Kawa ni sotte, sakura no ki ga narande iru.Cherry trees line the river.
  3. 計画けいかくに沿そって工事こうじが行おこなわれた。Keikaku ni sotte kōji ga okonawareta.The construction was carried out according to the plan.
Common mistakes
  • 指示を沿って動く。 → 指示に沿って動く。
    Use に, not を, before 沿って.
