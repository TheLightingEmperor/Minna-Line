JLPT N2Unit 20

～くらいなら

Rather than

How to build it
plain form + くらいならV る + くらいなら
What it means

Use plain form + くらいなら to say you would rather choose the second option than do the first.

  • The first option is so unpleasant that the other is better.
  • The second half states what you'd rather do.
  • It is a strong, emotional statement.
  • The example in the first half is often extreme.
A is so bad that B looks good.
Pattern flow
PartRole
A くらいならthe thing I dislike
B ほうがいいthe thing I choose
Example sentences
  1. あんな人ひとに頼たのむくらいなら、自分じぶんでやる。Anna hito ni tanomu kurai nara, jibun de yaru.Rather than ask someone like that, I'll do it myself.
  2. 謝あやまるくらいなら、最初さいしょからやらなければよかった。Ayamaru kurai nara, saisho kara yaranakereba yokatta.If I'd have to apologise, I shouldn't have done it at all.
  3. 一人ひとりで行いくくらいなら、行いかないほうがいい。Hitori de iku kurai nara, ikanai hō ga ii.Rather than go alone, it's better not to go.
Common mistakes
  • 謝るくらいに、やらない。 → 謝るくらいなら、やらない。
    Use なら, not に, after くらい.
