～に足る

Worth ...ing

How to build it
V dictionary form + に足るN + に足る
What it means

V dictionary form / N + に足る means worth doing or enough to deserve something.

  • It says something deserves or is good enough for the action.
  • Often with 信頼, 尊敬, 信じる.
  • The negative 取るに足らない means trivial.
Remember 取るに足らない (trivial) as the commonest set phrase.
Compare
PatternMeaning
に足るworth doing
に堪えないunbearable
Example sentences
  1. 彼かれは信頼しんらいするに足たる人物じんぶつだ。Kare wa shinrai suru ni taru jinbutsu da.He is a person worthy of trust.
  2. この調査ちょうさは信しんじるに足たる結果けっかを示しめしている。Kono chōsa wa shinjiru ni taru kekka o shimeshite iru.This survey shows results that can be trusted.
  3. 取とるに足たらない問題もんだいで悩なやむな。Toru ni tarinai mondai de nayamu na.Don't worry over trivial problems.
