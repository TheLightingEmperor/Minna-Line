JLPT N2Unit 24

～といっても

Although it's said

How to build it
plain form + といってもN + といっても
What it means

Use plain form + といっても to qualify a statement: the reality is smaller than the words suggest.

  • It qualifies a claim: yes, but only to a small degree.
  • The second half uses だけ, ぐらい or ほんの.
  • It is a spoken and written pattern.
  • It sounds modest or realistic.
Say yes — then cut it down to size.
Typical limiters
WordMeaning
だけonly
ぐらいabout
ほんのjust a little
Example sentences
  1. 料理りょうりができるといっても、簡単かんたんなものだけです。Ryōri ga dekiru to itte mo, kantan na mono dake desu.I can cook, but only simple things.
  2. 旅行りょこうに行いくといっても、近ちかくの温泉おんせんです。Ryokō ni iku to itte mo, chikaku no onsen desu.I'm going on a trip, but only to a nearby hot spring.
  3. 社長しゃちょうといっても、社員しゃいんは三人さんにんだけだ。Shachō to itte mo, shain wa sannin dake da.I'm the president, but there are only three employees.
Common mistakes
  • 料理ができるといっても、プロです。 → 料理ができるといっても、簡単なものだけです。
    The second half should shrink the claim.
