～けど

But (casual)

How to build it
Plain form + けどな-adjective / N + だけど
What it means

けど is the casual version of が ('but'). It attaches to the plain form: たかいけど, いったけど. Gotcha: nouns and な-adjectives need だ before けど — ひまだけど, not ひまけど. It can also be a soft lead-in, like 'I was wondering…'.

  • けど is the casual version of が, meaning but.
  • It can also be a soft lead-in, like I was wondering.
How each word type joins
Word typePlain formWith けどMeaning
Verbいったいったけどwent, but

Nounあめだあめだけどrainy, but
が vs けど
StyleJapaneseMeaning
Politeこの みせは たかいですが、おいしいです。This restaurant is expensive, but it's good.
Casualこの みせは たかいけど、おいしい。This restaurant is expensive, but it's good.
Example sentences
  1. この みせは たかいけど、おいしい。Kono mise wa takai kedo, oishii.This restaurant is expensive, but it's good.
  2. きのう デパートへ いったけど、なにも かわなかった。Kinō depāto e itta kedo, nani mo kawanakatta.I went to the department store yesterday, but didn't buy anything.
Common mistakes
  • あしたは ひまけど、あさっては いそがしい。 → あしたは ひまだけど、あさっては いそがしい。
    な-adjectives and nouns need だ before けど.
