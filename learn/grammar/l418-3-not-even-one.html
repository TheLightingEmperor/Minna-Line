JLPT N1Unit 18

～たりとも

Not even one ...

How to build it
一 + counter + たりとも + negative
What it means

Counter noun + たりとも + negative says that not even the smallest amount is allowed.

  • It forbids even the smallest unit.
  • It usually follows 一 plus a counter.
  • The ending must be negative.
Use it for 'not even one'.
Typical words
WordMeaning
一円one yen
一瞬a moment
Example sentences
  1. 一円いちえんたりとも無駄むだにできない。Ichien taritomo muda ni dekinai.I can't waste even one yen.
  2. 一瞬いっしゅんたりとも気きを抜ぬけない。Isshun taritomo ki o nukenai.I can't relax for even a moment.
  3. 一人ひとりたりとも逃にがしてはならない。Hitori taritomo nigashite wa naranai.You must not let a single one escape.
Common mistakes
  • 一円たりとも払う。 → 一円たりとも払わない。
    The ending must be negative.
