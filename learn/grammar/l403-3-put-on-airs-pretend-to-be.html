JLPT N1Unit 3

～ぶる

Put on airs; pretend to be ...

How to build it
N + ぶるna-adj stem + ぶる
What it means

N / na-adj stem + ぶる means to act like something, often falsely.

  • It describes acting a part that you are not.
  • The tone is mildly critical.
  • Common words: 偉ぶる, 大人ぶる, 上品ぶる.
Compare ぶる (put on) with めく (have a hint of).
Examples
WordMeaning
偉ぶるact important
善人ぶるact like a good person
Example sentences
  1. 彼かれはいつも偉えらぶって、誰だれの話はなしも聞きかない。Kare wa itsumo erabutte, dare no hanashi mo kikanai.He always acts important and listens to no one.
  2. 妹いもうとは大人おとなぶった口くちをきく。Imōto wa otonabutta kuchi o kiku.My sister talks as if she were a grown-up.
  3. 上品じょうひんぶっていても、育そだちは隠かくせない。Jōhinbutte ite mo, sodachi wa kakusenai.Even if you act refined, you can't hide your upbringing.
