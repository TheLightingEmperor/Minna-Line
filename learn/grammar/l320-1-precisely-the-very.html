JLPT N2Unit 20

～こそ

Precisely; the very

How to build it
N + こそV / adj + からこそ
What it means

Use N + こそ to stress that this one is the real one, or that it is exactly the reason.

  • It stresses that this and no other is the one.
  • The pattern からこそ stresses the very reason.
  • It can mean this time for sure.
  • こちらこそ is a polite reply.
Underline it: this is the one.
Common uses
PhraseMeaning
今年こそthis year, for sure
こちらこそI should be the one to say that
だからこそfor exactly that reason
Example sentences
  1. 今年ことしこそ、試験しけんに合格ごうかくしたい。Kotoshi koso, shiken ni gōkaku shitai.This year, for sure, I want to pass the exam.
  2. こちらこそ、よろしくお願ねがいします。Kochira koso, yoroshiku onegai shimasu.It is I who should say that — pleased to meet you.
  3. 努力どりょくしたからこそ、成功せいこうできたのだ。Doryoku shita kara koso, seikō dekita no da.It's precisely because I worked hard that I succeeded.
Common mistakes
  • 今年をこそがんばる。 → 今年こそがんばる。
    こそ follows the noun directly.
