JLPT N1Unit 9

～ずとも

Even without ...ing

How to build it
V ない-stem + ずともV せずとも (する)
What it means

V ない-stem (negative ず) + とも means you do not need to do it, or it does not matter if it is not done.

  • It means 'even if not'.
  • する becomes せずとも.
  • It is literary and old-fashioned.
Match it with 言わずもがな (needless to say).
Forms
VerbForm
言う言わずとも
するせずとも
Example sentences
  1. 言いわずとも、気持きもちは伝つたわった。Iwazu tomo, kimochi wa tsutawatta.Even without saying it, my feelings got through.
  2. 急いそがずとも、間まに合あうよ。Isogazu tomo, ma ni au yo.You don't need to hurry; you'll make it.
  3. 見みずとも分わかるほど、結果けっかは明あきらかだった。Mizu tomo wakaru hodo, kekka wa akiraka datta.The outcome was clear enough to know without looking.
Common mistakes
  • しずとも分かる。 → せずとも分かる。
    する becomes せず, not しず.
