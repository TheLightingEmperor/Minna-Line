JLPT N2Unit 3

～っぽい

-ish; tending to be

How to build it
N + っぽいV ます-stem + っぽいいA stem + っぽい
What it means

Use N / V ます-stem / adjective stem + っぽい to say something has a quality, or tends to be so.

  • It means -ish, or having a quality of N.
  • With a verb it means being apt to do it.
  • The feeling is casual and often critical.
  • It behaves like an い-adjective: っぽくて, っぽかった.
Say it about people and things in daily speech, not in formal writing.
Examples
WordMeaning
子どもっぽいchildish
あきっぽいeasily bored
白っぽいwhitish
水っぽいwatery
Example sentences
  1. 弟おとうとは飽あきっぽくて、何なにをしても続つづかない。Otōto wa akippokute, nani o shite mo tsuzukanai.My brother gets bored easily and sticks to nothing.
  2. このコーヒーは水みずっぽくて、味あじが薄うすい。Kono kōhī wa mizuppokute, aji ga usui.This coffee is watery and tastes thin.
  3. 黒くろっぽいコートを探さがしている。Kuroppoi kōto o sagashite iru.I'm looking for a blackish coat.
Common mistakes
  • 怒るっぽい人 → 怒りっぽい人
    Use the ます-stem before っぽい.
