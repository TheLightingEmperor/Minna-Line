JLPT N2Unit 3

～がちだ

Tends to; apt to

How to build it
V ます-stem + がちだN + がちだ
What it means

Use V ます-stem / N + がちだ for something that tends to happen, usually an unwelcome tendency.

  • It describes something that tends to happen.
  • The tendency is usually unwelcome.
  • Nouns work too, like 病気がち and 曇りがち.
  • You can say がちな N or がちになる.
If it is a habit you would rather not have, がち fits.
Common combinations
WordMeaning
忘れがちapt to forget
遅れがちapt to be late
病気がちoften ill
曇りがちoften cloudy
Example sentences
  1. 冬ふゆは風邪かぜを引ひきがちなので、気きをつけてください。Fuyu wa kaze o hikigachi na node, ki o tsukete kudasai.In winter people tend to catch colds, so take care.
  2. 彼かれは約束やくそくを忘わすれがちだ。Kare wa yakusoku o wasuregachi da.He tends to forget promises.
  3. 最近さいきん、家いえにこもりがちになった。Saikin, ie ni komorigachi ni natta.Lately I've tended to stay shut up at home.
Common mistakes
  • 彼は忘れるがちだ。 → 彼は忘れがちだ。
    Use the ます-stem before がち.
