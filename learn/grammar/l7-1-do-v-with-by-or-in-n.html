Tool で V

Do V with, by or in N

How to build it
Tool で VLanguage で V
What it means

で also marks the tool, method or language you use: はしで たべます (eat with chopsticks), にほんごで はなします (speak in Japanese). It's the same particle as the place で — context tells you which. Don't use と here: と means 'together with a person'.

  • で marks the tool, method or language you use.
  • It is the same で as the place で. Context shows which.

Example sentences
  1. はしで ごはんを たべます。Hashi de gohan o tabemasu.I eat rice with chopsticks.
  2. パソコンで メールを おくります。Pasokon de mēru o okurimasu.I send emails on my computer.
  3. にほんごで レポートを かきます。Nihongo de repōto o kakimasu.I write the report in Japanese.
Common mistakes
  • はしと ごはんを たべます。 → はしで ごはんを たべます。
    と means with a person. Tools take で.
