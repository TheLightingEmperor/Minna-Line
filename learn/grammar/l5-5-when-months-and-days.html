いつ and dates

When? Months and days

How to build it
いつ V か～がつ ～にち
What it means

いつ means 'when' and never takes に. Dates are ～がつ (month) + ～にち (day), with irregular readings: ついたち (1st), ふつか, みっか, よっか, いつか, むいか, なのか, ようか, ここのか, とおか, はつか (20th). Months: 4 = しがつ, 7 = しちがつ, 9 = くがつ.

  • いつ means when and never takes に.
  • A date in the answer does take に.
  • Most days are a number plus にち. Learn the special ones below.

Example sentences
  1. いつ にほんへ きましたか。…さんがつ じゅうごにちに きました。Itsu Nihon e kimashita ka. …San-gatsu jūgo-nichi ni kimashita.When did you come to Japan? — I came on March 15th.
  2. たんじょうびは いつですか。…ろくがつ ようかです。Tanjōbi wa itsu desu ka. …Roku-gatsu yōka desu.When is your birthday? — June 8th.
Common mistakes
  • いつに にほんへ きましたか。 → いつ にほんへ きましたか。
    いつ never takes に.
