～や否や

The instant ...

How to build it
V dictionary form + や否やV dictionary form + や
What it means

V dictionary form + や否や describes a second action that happens immediately after the first.

  • It is a written, formal pattern.
  • The second action follows with no delay.
  • It is usually about the past, not a wish.
Same speed as が早いか, but it sounds more formal.

FormExample

Example sentences
  1. 彼女かのじょは部屋へやに入はいるや否いなや、窓まどを開あけた。Kanojo wa heya ni hairu ya ina ya, mado o aketa.The instant she entered the room, she opened the window.
  2. 試合しあいが終おわるや否いなや、観客かんきゃくが一斉いっせいに立たち上あがった。Shiai ga owaru ya ina ya, kankyaku ga issei ni tachiagatta.The instant the match ended, the crowd all stood up.
  3. 社長しゃちょうが話はなし終おえるや否いなや、質問しつもんが相次あいついだ。Shachō ga hanashi oeru ya ina ya, shitsumon ga aitsuida.The instant the president finished speaking, questions came one after another.
Common mistakes
  • 食べるや否や、寝なさい。 → 食べるや否や、寝てしまった。
    Do not use commands or wishes in the second half.
