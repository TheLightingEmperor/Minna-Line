JLPT N2Unit 17

～上に

Moreover; on top of that

How to build it
plain form + 上にN の / なA な + 上に
What it means

Use plain form + 上に to add one more point of the same direction, good or bad.

  • It adds a second point of the same kind.
  • Both points are good, or both are bad.
  • The second half often has も or まで.
  • It is close to ばかりでなく.
Pile on one more point of the same kind.
Forms
TypeExample
Verb降っている上に
I-adjective安い上に
Na-adjective便利な上に
Noun雨の上に
Example sentences
  1. このホテルは安やすい上うえに、サービスもいい。Kono hoteru wa yasui ue ni, sābisu mo ii.This hotel is cheap, and the service is good too.
  2. 今日きょうは雨あめが降ふっている上うえに、風かぜも強つよい。Kyō wa ame ga futte iru ue ni, kaze mo tsuyoi.Today it's raining, and on top of that it's windy.
  3. 彼女かのじょは忙いそがしい上うえに、病気びょうきまでしてしまった。Kanojo wa isogashii ue ni, byōki made shite shimatta.She was busy and even fell ill on top of it.
Common mistakes
  • 安い上に、高い。 → 安い上に、おいしい。
    Both points need to point the same direction.
