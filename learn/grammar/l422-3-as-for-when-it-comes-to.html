JLPT N1Unit 22

～に至っては

As for ...; when it comes to ...

How to build it
N + に至っては
What it means

N + に至っては singles out the most extreme example among several, usually something bad.

  • It singles out an extreme example.
  • The extreme example is usually bad or surprising.
  • Several comparable items come before it.
Say the lesser cases first, then the worst one with に至っては.
Compare
PatternFeel
に至ってはextreme example

Example sentences
  1. 兄あにも弟おとうとも勉強べんきょうしないが、末すえっ子こに至いたっては学校がっこうにも行いかない。Ani mo otōto mo benkyō shinai ga, suekko ni itatte wa gakkō ni mo ikanai.Neither brother studies, and the youngest doesn't even go to school.
  2. この店みせは値段ねだんが高たかい。特とくにワインに至いたっては一本いっぽん五万円ごまんえんもする。Kono mise wa nedan ga takai. Toku ni wain ni itatte wa ippon goman'en mo suru.This shop is expensive. As for the wine, a bottle costs 50,000 yen.
  3. 彼かれの部屋へやは散ちらかっていて、机つくえの上うえに至いたっては足あしの踏ふみ場ばもない。Kare no heya wa chirakatte ite, tsukue no ue ni itatte wa ashi no fumiba mo nai.His room is messy, and as for the desk, there's nowhere to step.
