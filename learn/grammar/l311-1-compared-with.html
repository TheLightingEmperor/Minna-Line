JLPT N2Unit 11

～に比べて

Compared with

How to build it
N + に比べてN + に比べるとN + と比べて
What it means

Use N + に比べて(比べると) to compare one thing with another.

  • The thing after に is the standard you compare with.
  • The result is a difference: higher, lower, more, less.
  • に比べると is a gentle variant.
  • と比べて has the same meaning.
Put A next to B. The gap is the message.
Variants
FormExample
に比べて去年に比べて
に比べると去年に比べると
と比べて去年と比べて
Example sentences
  1. 去年きょねんに比くらべて、物価ぶっかが上あがった。Kyonen ni kurabete, bukka ga agatta.Prices have risen compared with last year.
  2. 都会とかいに比くらべて、田舎いなかは家賃やちんが安やすい。Tokai ni kurabete, inaka wa yachin ga yasui.Rent is cheaper in the countryside than in the city.
  3. 私わたしの給料きゅうりょうは、兄あにに比くらべて少すくない。Watashi no kyūryō wa, ani ni kurabete sukunai.My salary is lower than my brother's.
Common mistakes
  • 去年をくらべて多い。 → 去年に比べて多い。
    Use に before 比べて.
