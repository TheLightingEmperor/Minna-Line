お～ (polite prefix)

Polite お before the listener's things

How to build it
お + N
What it means

お is added to some words to show respect when talking about the listener's things: おくに (your country), おなまえ (your name). Never use it for your own things — not 'わたしの おくに'. A few words always carry お, like おみやげ and おてあらい.

  • A prefix is a sound added to the front of a word.
  • お shows respect for the listener's things.
  • Never use it for your own things.
WordMeaningWhen
おくにYour countryOnly about the listener
おなまえYour nameOnly about the listener
おみやげSouvenirAlways has it
おてあらいRestroomAlways has it
Example sentences
  1. しつれいですが、おなまえは？…ベイカーです。Shitsurei desu ga, o-namae wa? …Beikā desu.Excuse me, but what's your name? — Baker.
  2. おくには どちらですか。…タイです。O-kuni wa dochira desu ka. …Tai desu.Where are you from? — Thailand.
  3. おてあらいは どこですか。O-tearai wa doko desu ka.Where is the restroom?
Common mistakes
  • わたしの おくには インドです。 → わたしの くには インドです。
    Never use お for your own things.
