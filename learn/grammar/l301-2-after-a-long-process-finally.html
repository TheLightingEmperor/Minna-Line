JLPT N2Unit 1

～た末に

After a long process, finally

How to build it
V た + 末にN の + 末に
What it means

Use V た / N の + 末に when a result comes after a long time of effort, worry or discussion.

  • The process is long and hard, and the result comes at the end.
  • The result can be good or bad.
  • The second half is a past fact or a decision.
  • Typical verbs are 悩む, 考える and 話し合う.
Picture a long road. The result sits at the very end of it.
Compare with あげく
PatternResult
～た末にGood or bad
～あげくMostly bad
Example sentences
  1. 三日間みっかかん悩なやんだ末すえに、会社かいしゃを辞やめることにした。Mikkakan nayanda sue ni, kaisha o yameru koto ni shita.After worrying for three days, I decided to quit my job.
  2. 長ながい議論ぎろんの末すえに、新あたらしい案あんが決きまった。Nagai giron no sue ni, atarashii an ga kimatta.After a long debate, a new plan was decided.
  3. 苦労くろうした末すえに、やっと試験しけんに合格ごうかくした。Kurō shita sue ni, yatto shiken ni gōkaku shita.After much hardship, I finally passed the exam.
Common mistakes
  • 議論する末に、決まった。 → 議論した末に、決まった。
    Use the た-form before 末に.
