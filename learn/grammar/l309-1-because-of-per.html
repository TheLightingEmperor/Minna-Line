JLPT N2Unit 9

～につき

Because of; per

How to build it
N + につきN + につき (per)
What it means

Use N + につき for a formal reason on signs and notices. It also means 'per' with counts.

  • It gives a formal reason on signs and notices.
  • With a count, it means per: 一人につき.
  • It sounds stiff and is rarely spoken.
  • The nouns are usually short: 雨天, 工事中, 改装中.
Read it on any sign and think 'because'.
Two uses
JapaneseMeaning
改装中につき休業closed due to renovation
一人につき一つone per person
Example sentences
  1. 工事中こうじちゅうにつき、通行止つうこうどめです。Kōjichū ni tsuki, tsūkōdome desu.Closed to traffic due to roadworks.
  2. 本日ほんじつは休業きゅうぎょうにつき、入はいれません。Honjitsu wa kyūgyō ni tsuki, hairemasen.Closed today, so you cannot enter.
  3. お一人ひとりにつき、三枚さんまいまでです。Ohitori ni tsuki, sanmai made desu.Up to three sheets per person.
Common mistakes
  • 雨につき、中止になった。 → 雨天につき、中止します。
    につき goes with formal nouns like 雨天, not a plain 雨.
