～て以来

Ever since

How to build it
V て + 以来N + 以来
What it means

Use V て + 以来 to say a state has continued from one past point until now.

  • The first action is a clear past point.
  • The second half continues until now.
  • It often goes with ずっと or ～ていない.
  • It doesn't fit very recent events like きのう.
Think of a long line from a past point to today.
てから and て以来
PatternTime feeling
てからAfter that, any result
て以来Since then, continuing until now
Example sentences
  1. 去年きょねんギターを始はじめて以来いらい、毎日まいにちひいている。Kyonen gitā o hajimete irai, mainichi hiite iru.Ever since I took up the guitar last year, I've played every day.
  2. そつぎょうして以来いらい、先生せんせいに会あっていない。Sotsugyō shite irai, sensei ni atte inai.I haven't seen my teacher since I graduated.
  3. 大学だいがく入学にゅうがく以来いらい、ずっと同おなじクラブに入はいっている。Daigaku nyūgaku irai, zutto onaji kurabu ni haitte iru.Since entering university, I've been in the same club.
Common mistakes
  • きのう来て以来、頭が痛い。 → きのうから、頭が痛い。
    て以来 needs a longer stretch of time.
  • 始めた以来、続けている。 → 始めて以来、続けている。
    Use the て-form.
