N1 の N2 (topic / owner) and N1 の

About N1, N1's thing, it's N1's

How to build it
N1 の N2N1 のです
What it means

の can show what something is about (コンピューターの ほん = a book about computers) or who owns it (わたしの かさ = my umbrella). When the thing is already clear, drop it: わたしのです = 'It's mine'. Ask the owner with だれの. (Don't drop the noun when it's a person.)

  • Drop the second noun when it is already clear.
  • Never drop the noun when it is a person.
What の can show
JobExampleEnglish
What it is aboutコンピューターの ほんa book about computers
Who owns itわたしの かさmy umbrella
Owner, noun droppedわたしのです。It's mine.
Asking the ownerだれの かさですか。Whose umbrella is it?
Example sentences
  1. これは コンピューターの ほんです。Kore wa konpyūtā no hon desu.This is a book about computers.
  2. これは わたしの かさです。Kore wa watashi no kasa desu.This is my umbrella.
  3. あの かばんは だれのですか。…さとうさんのです。Ano kaban wa dare no desu ka. …Satō-san no desu.Whose is that bag? — It's Ms. Sato's.
Common mistakes
  • あの ひとは ふじだいがくのです。 → あの ひとは ふじだいがくの がくせいです。
    You can't drop a noun that means a person.
