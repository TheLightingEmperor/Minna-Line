～がち

Tend to (usually something bad)

How to build it
V ます-stem + がちN + がち～がちな + N
What it means

Use ～がち to say something tends to happen, usually something undesirable.

  • This often happens, even if you don't intend it.
  • Usually it's something undesirable.
  • Attach it to the ます-stem or a few nouns.
  • Fixed nouns include 病気がち and 留守がち.
Before a noun, use がちな: ありがちなまちがい (a very common mistake).
Common words
JapaneseMeaning
忘れがちTend to forget
遅れがちTend to be late
病気がちOften ill
留守がちOften away from home
Example sentences
  1. 寒さむい日ひは、家いえにこもりがちになる。Samui hi wa, ie ni komorigachi ni naru.On cold days I tend to stay shut in at home.
  2. 忙いそがしいと、朝あさごはんを抜ぬきがちだ。Isogashii to, asagohan o nukigachi da.When I'm busy, I tend to skip breakfast.
  3. 父ちちは仕事しごとで留守るすがちです。Chichi wa shigoto de rusugachi desu.My father is often away from home for work.
Common mistakes
  • 忘れるがちです。 → 忘れがちです。
    Use the ます-stem.
  • 彼は勉強しがちだ。 → 彼はよく勉強する。
    Good habits don't take がち.
