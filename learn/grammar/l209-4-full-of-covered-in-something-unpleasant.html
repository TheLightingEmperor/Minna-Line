JLPT N3Unit 9

～だらけ

Full of, covered in (something unpleasant)

How to build it
N + だらけN + だらけの + NN + だらけだ / だらけで / だらけになる
What it means

Use noun + だらけ to say something is covered in or full of something unpleasant.

  • It means too many, or all over the surface.
  • It's usually something unpleasant.
  • Only nouns come before it.
Common words: 間違いだらけ, ごみだらけ, どろだらけ.
Saying full of
WordFeelingExample
だらけToo many, unpleasantごみだらけ
いっぱいNeutral, full人でいっぱい
ばかりOnly thatごみばかり
Example sentences
  1. この作文さくぶんは間違まちがいだらけだ。Kono sakubun wa machigai darake da.This essay is full of mistakes.
  2. 部屋へやがごみだらけで、歩あるけない。Heya ga gomi darake de, arukenai.The room is so full of rubbish that I can't walk.
  3. 子こどもたちはどろだらけになって帰かえってきた。Kodomotachi wa doro darake ni natte kaette kita.The kids came home covered in mud.
Common mistakes
  • 部屋が花だらけで、きれいだ。 → 部屋が花でいっぱいで、きれいだ。
    だらけ sounds unpleasant.
  • きたないだらけの部屋 → ごみだらけの部屋
    Only nouns come before it.
