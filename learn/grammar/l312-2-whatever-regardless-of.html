JLPT N2Unit 12

～を問わず

Whatever; regardless of

How to build it
N + を問わず
What it means

Use N + を問わず when a wide range of kinds or types are all included.

  • It means regardless of, covering all kinds.
  • Nouns suggest a range: 昼夜, 男女, 年齢, 国籍.
  • It's common in job ads and notices.
  • The verb 問う means to ask or care about.
The doors are open to every type.
Common nouns
NounMeaning
昼夜を問わずday and night
季節を問わずin every season
年齢を問わずregardless of age
Example sentences
  1. 経験けいけんを問とわず、誰だれでも歓迎かんげいします。Keiken o towazu, dare demo kangei shimasu.Everyone is welcome regardless of experience.
  2. この店みせは、昼夜ちゅうやを問とわず、お客きゃくさんが多おおい。Kono mise wa, chūya o towazu, okyaku-san ga ōi.This shop has many customers day and night.
  3. 男女だんじょを問とわず、人気にんきのある歌手かしゅだ。Danjo o towazu, ninki no aru kashu da.He's a singer popular with men and women alike.
Common mistakes
  • 年齢に問わず参加できる。 → 年齢を問わず参加できる。
    Use を before 問わず.
