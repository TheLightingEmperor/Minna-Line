JLPT N1Unit 24

～かぎりだ

Extremely ...

How to build it
i-adj + 限りだna-adj な + 限りだ
What it means

Feeling word + 限りだ expresses a strong emotion felt at that moment.

  • It shows a feeling at its peak.
  • Words: 嬉しい, 心強い, 残念な, 羨ましい.
  • It is used for the speaker's own emotion.
Use it when you want the feeling to sound strong.
Typical words
WordMeaning
心強いreassuring
羨ましいenvious
Example sentences
  1. 君きみに会あえて、嬉うれしい限かぎりだ。Kimi ni aete, ureshii kagiri da.I'm extremely happy to see you.
  2. 一人ひとりで知しらない町まちにいて、心細こころぼそい限かぎりだった。Hitori de shiranai machi ni ite, kokorobosoi kagiri datta.Alone in an unknown town, I felt extremely uneasy.
  3. 隣となりの成功せいこうを見みて、羨うらやましい限かぎりだ。Tonari no seikō o mite, urayamashii kagiri da.Seeing my neighbour's success, I'm extremely envious.
Common mistakes
  • 高い限りだ。 → 嬉しい限りだ。
    限りだ needs an emotion word.
