JLPT N1Unit 11

～はおろか

Let alone ...; not even

How to build it
N1 + はおろか + N2 + も + negative
What it means

N + はおろか, A negative expresses that even the easy thing is not true, so the harder is certainly not.

  • N1 is the bigger or easier one, N2 the smaller.
  • The ending is negative.
  • It often shows frustration or surprise.
Think of a staircase: the big step is out, so is the small one.
Compare
PatternFeel
はおろかlet alone, negative
はもちろんof course, positive
Example sentences
  1. 彼かれは漢字かんじはおろか、ひらがなも書かけない。Kare wa kanji wa oroka, hiragana mo kakenai.He can't even write hiragana, let alone kanji.
  2. 海外かいがいはおろか、県外けんがいにも出でたことがない。Kaigai wa oroka, kengai ni mo deta koto ga nai.I've never been out of the prefecture, let alone abroad.
  3. 朝あさごはんはおろか、水みずも飲のんでいない。Asagohan wa oroka, mizu mo nonde inai.I haven't even had water, let alone breakfast.
Common mistakes
  • 日本語はおろか、英語も話せる。 → 日本語はおろか、英語も話せない。
    はおろか needs a negative ending.
