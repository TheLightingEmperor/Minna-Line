Plain + のに

Although ~, and yet ~

How to build it
V / いA plain + のになA / N + な + のに
What it means

～のに joins two facts when the second one goes against what you expected, and it carries surprise or disappointment.

  • The result is the opposite of what you expected.
  • It shows surprise, regret or a complaint.
  • Nouns and な-adjectives take な.
Say it with a sigh. If you wouldn't sigh, you may want が instead.

Example sentences
  1. 春はるなのに、とても さむいです。Haru na noni, totemo samui desu.Even though it's spring, it's very cold.
  2. モチは 朝あさ たくさん 食たべたのに、もう おなかが すいて います。Mochi wa asa takusan tabeta noni, mō onaka ga suite imasu.Mochi ate a lot this morning but is hungry already.
  3. この 車くるまは 高たかかったのに、すぐ こわれました。Kono kuruma wa takakatta noni, sugu kowaremashita.This car was expensive, and yet it broke straight away.
Common mistakes
  • はるのに、さむいです。 → はるなのに、さむいです。
    A noun needs な before のに.
  • げんきだのに、やすみました。 → げんきなのに、やすみました。
    Use な, not だ.
