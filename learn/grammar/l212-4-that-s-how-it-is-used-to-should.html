JLPT N3Unit 12

～ものだ

That's how it is, used to, should

How to build it
V る / いA + ものだV た + ものだV る + ものではない
What it means

Use ～ものだ to state a general truth, or with V た to remember something you often did in the past.

  • General truth: that's just how things are.
  • With た: a past habit remembered fondly.
  • Social norm: this is what people should do.
  • ～ものではない means one shouldn't.
Add よく and 昔 to sound nostalgic.
Three uses
UseJapaneseMeaning
Truth時間は早くたつものだ。Time passes quickly.
Memoryよく遊んだものだ。I often used to play.
Norm人の物は大切にするものだ。You should take care of others' things.
Example sentences
  1. 人ひとは年としをとると、昔むかしのことを思おもい出だすものだ。Hito wa toshi o toru to, mukashi no koto o omoidasu mono da.As people age, they tend to think back on the past.
  2. 子こどものころ、よくこの川かわで泳およいだものだ。Kodomo no koro, yoku kono kawa de oyoida mono da.As a child, I often swam in this river.
  3. 目上めうえの人ひとには、ていねいに話はなすものだ。Meue no hito ni wa, teinei ni hanasu mono da.You should speak politely to your elders.
Common mistakes
  • きのう川で泳いだものだ。 → きのう川で泳いだ。
    It needs a past habit, not one event.
  • 私は毎朝コーヒーを飲むものだ。 → 私は毎朝コーヒーを飲む。
    Don't use it for a personal habit.
