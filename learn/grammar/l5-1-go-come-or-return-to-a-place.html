Place へ いきます / きます / かえります

Go, come or return to a place

How to build it
Place へ いきます / きます / かえります
What it means

へ marks the direction or destination of movement and is read 'e', not 'he'. いきます = go, きます = come (toward where the speaker is), かえります = go home / go back. You will also hear に used the same way.

  • へ marks where you are going. Read it as e, not he.
  • You will also hear に used the same way.
Three movement verbs
VerbMeaningExampleEnglish
いきますGoきょうとへ いきます。I'll go to Kyoto.
きますCome toward the speakerにほんへ きました。I came to Japan.
かえりますGo home, go backうちへ かえります。I'll go home.
Example sentences
  1. きょうとへ いきます。Kyōto e ikimasu.I'll go to Kyoto.
  2. せんげつ にほんへ きました。Sengetsu Nihon e kimashita.I came to Japan last month.
  3. きのう うちへ かえりました。Kinō uchi e kaerimashita.I went home yesterday.
