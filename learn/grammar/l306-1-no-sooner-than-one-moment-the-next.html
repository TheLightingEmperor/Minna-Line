～かと思うと

No sooner than; one moment... the next

How to build it
V た + かと思うとV た + かと思ったら
What it means

Use V た + かと思うと for two quick or opposite events that you saw happen.

  • It describes two events that follow each other fast.
  • The second event is often a surprise or the opposite.
  • Only for events you saw, not your own plans.
  • かと思ったら is the spoken form.
One thing, then — surprise! — the next thing.
Close patterns
PatternFeeling
かと思うとsurprise at fast change
たとたんjust at that moment
Example sentences
  1. 雨あめが降ふり出だしたかと思おもうと、すぐにやんだ。Ame ga furidashita ka to omou to, sugu ni yanda.No sooner had it started to rain than it stopped.
  2. 弟おとうとは帰かえってきたかと思おもうと、すぐ出でかけていった。Otōto wa kaette kita ka to omou to, sugu dekakete itta.My brother came home, then went right out again.
  3. さっきまで泣ないていたかと思おもったら、もう笑わらっている。Sakki made naite ita ka to omottara, mō waratte iru.One moment crying, the next he's laughing.
Common mistakes
  • 私は着いたかと思うと、帰ろうと思った。 → 弟は着いたかと思うと、帰ってしまった。
    The second half is an observed fact, not your plan.
