JLPT N1Unit 22

～をふまえて

Based on ...; taking into account

How to build it
N + をふまえてN + をふまえた + N
What it means

N + をふまえて means taking a fact or past result as a basis for the next step.

  • N is an already known fact or result.
  • The next action builds on it.
  • It is common in reports and policy.
Use it with 結果, 反省, 経験 and 現状.
Compare

Example sentences
  1. 前回ぜんかいの反省はんせいをふまえて、計画けいかくを立たてた。Zenkai no hansei o fumaete, keikaku o tateta.Based on the lessons of last time, I made the plan.
  2. 調査ちょうさの結果けっかをふまえて、方針ほうしんを決きめる。Chōsa no kekka o fumaete, hōshin o kimeru.We will decide the policy based on the survey results.
  3. 現状げんじょうをふまえた上うえで、議論ぎろんしたい。Genjō o fumaeta ue de, giron shitai.I'd like to discuss, taking the current state into account.
