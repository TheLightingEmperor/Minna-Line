JLPT N3Unit 7

～かわりに

Instead of, in exchange for

How to build it
N の + かわりにV / いA plain + かわりになA な + かわりに
What it means

Use ～かわりに when one thing replaces another, or when something good comes with something bad.

  • Replacement: B instead of A.
  • Exchange: you do something in return.
  • Balance: a good point comes with a bad point.
  • It is also written 代わりに.
Use it to make a deal: AするかわりにBして。
Three uses
UseJapaneseMeaning
Replacement父のかわりに、私が行く。I'll go in place of my father.
Exchange教えるかわりに、手伝って。I'll teach you, so help me in return.
Balance安いかわりに、小さい。It's cheap, but small.
Example sentences
  1. 現金げんきんのかわりに、カードで払はらった。Genkin no kawari ni, kādo de haratta.I paid by card instead of cash.
  2. この店みせは値段ねだんが安やすいかわりに、配達はいたつはしてくれない。Kono mise wa nedan ga yasui kawari ni, haitatsu wa shite kurenai.This shop is cheap, but in exchange it doesn't deliver.
  3. 宿題しゅくだいを手伝てつだうかわりに、昼ひるごはんをおごって。Shukudai o tetsudau kawari ni, hirugohan o ogotte.I'll help with your homework. In exchange, buy me lunch.
Common mistakes
  • ペンかわりに、えんぴつを使う。 → ペンのかわりに、えんぴつを使う。
    Nouns need の.
