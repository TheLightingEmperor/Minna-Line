JLPT N2Unit 7

～に基づいて

Based on

How to build it
N + に基づいてN + に基づく / に基づいた + N
What it means

Use N + に基づいて for judging or acting according to facts, laws, rules or data.

  • The noun is a firm base: law, facts, data, rules.
  • It sounds formal and serious.
  • Use に基づいた or に基づく before a noun.
  • It is a base to judge or act on, not a story to retell.
Firm ground under your feet: law, fact or data.

Example sentences
  1. 法律ほうりつに基もとづいて、判断はんだんする。Hōritsu ni motozuite, handan suru.We judge on the basis of the law.
  2. 事実じじつに基もとづいた報道ほうどうが大切たいせつだ。Jijitsu ni motozuita hōdō ga taisetsu da.News based on facts is important.
  3. データに基もとづいて、計画けいかくを立たてた。Dēta ni motozuite, keikaku o tateta.We drew up the plan based on the data.
Common mistakes
  • 事実を基づいた話 → 事実に基づいた話
    Use に before 基づく.
