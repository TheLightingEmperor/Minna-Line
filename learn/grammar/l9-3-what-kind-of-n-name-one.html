どんな N (naming an item)

What kind of N? Name one.

How to build it
どんな N が すきですかN が すきです
What it means

In Lesson 9, どんな + noun asks someone to name a specific item from a category: どんな スポーツが すきですか → サッカーが すきです. Answer with the item itself, not はい/いいえ.

  • どんな must come before a noun.
  • Here it asks for a specific item from a category.
  • Answer with the item itself, not はい or いいえ.
Two kinds of answer

8A descriptionならは どんな まちですか。ふるい まちです。What kind of town is Nara? An old town.
