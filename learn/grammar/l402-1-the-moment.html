～が早いか

The moment ...

How to build it
V dictionary form + が早いかV た + が早いか
What it means

V dictionary form + が早いか describes one action that is followed at once by another.

  • Two actions happen with almost no gap.
  • The second action is usually sudden or unexpected.
  • The second half is not a wish or a command.
Imagine a stopwatch: the gap between the two actions is zero.
Similar patterns
PatternFeel
が早いかwritten, vivid

Example sentences
  1. 彼かれは席せきに着つくが早はやいか、寝ねてしまった。Kare wa seki ni tsuku ga hayai ka, nete shimatta.The moment he sat down, he fell asleep.
  2. ベルが鳴なるが早はやいか、生徒せいとたちは教室きょうしつを飛とび出だした。Beru ga naru ga hayai ka, seito-tachi wa kyōshitsu o tobidashita.The moment the bell rang, the students dashed out of the classroom.
  3. 赤あかん坊ぼうは母親ははおやの顔かおを見みるが早はやいか、泣なき止やんだ。Akanbō wa hahaoya no kao o miru ga hayai ka, nakiyanda.The baby stopped crying the instant it saw its mother's face.
Common mistakes
  • 帰るが早いか、寝たいです。 → 帰るが早いか、寝てしまった。
    The second half must be a real action, not a wish.
