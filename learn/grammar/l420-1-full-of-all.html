JLPT N1Unit 20

～ずくめ

Full of ...; all ...

How to build it
N + ずくめN + ずくめの + N
What it means

N + ずくめ means a state filled with only one thing, such as good news or black clothes.

  • Everything is the same kind.
  • Common: 黒ずくめ, いいことずくめ, 規則ずくめ.
  • It can be good or bad.
Learn 黒ずくめ and いいことずくめ first.
Compare

Example sentences
  1. 今日きょうはいいことずくめの一日いちにちだった。Kyō wa ii koto zukume no ichinichi datta.Today was a day full of good things.
  2. 彼かれは黒くろずくめの服装ふくそうで現あらわれた。Kare wa kurozukume no fukusō de arawareta.He appeared dressed all in black.
  3. この旅行りょこうは規則きそくずくめで疲つかれた。Kono ryokō wa kisoku zukume de tsukareta.This trip was all rules and tiring.
