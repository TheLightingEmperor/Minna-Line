～くらい・ぐらい

About; to the extent that; at least

How to build it
number + くらいV plain + くらい + stateN + くらい
What it means

くらい gives a rough amount, a degree, or a small minimum that is treated as easy.

  • くらい and ぐらい are interchangeable.
  • It can mark a rough number.
  • It can show degree, like ほど.
  • After a noun it can play something down.
ほど sounds a little stronger, so choose くらい when you are playing something down.

Example sentences
  1. 雪ゆきは五十ごじゅっセンチくらい積つもった。Yuki wa gojussenchi kurai tsumotta.About fifty centimetres of snow piled up.
  2. 台風たいふうの夜よるは、家いえがゆれるくらい風かぜが吹ふいた。Taifū no yoru wa, ie ga yureru kurai kaze ga fuita.On the night of the typhoon, the wind blew so hard the house shook.
  3. 少すこしの雨あめくらいなら、かさはいらない。Sukoshi no ame kurai nara, kasa wa iranai.For a little rain like that, I do not need an umbrella.
Common mistakes
  • メールほど書いてください。 → メールくらい書いてください。
    Only くらい can mean 'at least'.
