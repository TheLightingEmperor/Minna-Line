JLPT N2Unit 10

～以上は／上は

Since; given that

How to build it
plain form + 以上(は)V た + 上は
What it means

Use plain form + 以上(は) or V た + 上は when something has been decided and you must take the consequence.

  • It means because it is so, there is no turning back.
  • The second half is a duty, resolve or advice.
  • 上は needs the た-form and is more formal.
  • It is close to からには.
Once it is decided, you play it out.
Compare
PatternRegister
からにはspoken and written
以上はa bit formal
上はformal, written
Example sentences
  1. 決きめた以上いじょうは、最後さいごまでやり通とおす。Kimeta ijō wa, saigo made yaritōsu.Having decided, I will see it through to the end.
  2. 契約けいやくした上うえは、守まもる義務ぎむがある。Keiyaku shita ue wa, mamoru gimu ga aru.Having signed the contract, you have a duty to keep it.
  3. 大人おとなである以上いじょう、自分じぶんで責任せきにんを取とるべきだ。Otona de aru ijō, jibun de sekinin o toru beki da.Since you are an adult, you should take your own responsibility.
Common mistakes
  • 決めたの以上は、後悔しない。 → 決めた以上は、後悔しない。
    Put 以上 straight after the plain form, with no の.
