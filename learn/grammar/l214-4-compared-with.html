JLPT N3Unit 14

N + にくらべて

Compared with

How to build it
N + にくらべてN + にくらべると
What it means

にくらべて sets up a comparison with the noun as the standard.

  • The noun before に is the standard you measure against.
  • The result is usually a state or a change.
  • にくらべると means the same.
  • Later you will also see it written with a kanji.
Use it to describe trends: 去年にくらべて is a favourite opening in news reports.
より or にくらべて
JapaneseMeaning
AはBより高いA is more expensive than B
AはBにくらべて高いA is expensive compared with B
Example sentences
  1. 去年きょねんにくらべて、今年ことしは米こめの生産せいさんが増ふえた。Kyonen ni kurabete, kotoshi wa kome no seisan ga fueta.Compared with last year, rice production has risen this year.
  2. プラスチックはガラスにくらべてかるい。Purasuchikku wa garasu ni kurabete karui.Plastic is light compared with glass.
  3. 昔むかしにくらべると、工場こうじょうの仕事しごとは楽らくになった。Mukashi ni kuraberu to, kōjō no shigoto wa raku ni natta.Compared with the past, factory work has become easier.
Common mistakes
  • ガラスをくらべてかるい。 → ガラスにくらべてかるい。
    を + くらべる means you do the comparing. Use に for the standard.
