JLPT N1Unit 1

～を皮切りに

Starting with ... (and more follow)

How to build it
N + を皮切りにN + を皮切りとして
What it means

N + を皮切りに means that starting with N, many similar things follow one after another.

  • The first event starts a chain of similar events.
  • The second half usually has 続く, 広がる or 相次ぐ.
  • It sounds like news writing.
Picture the first domino: it falls, then many others fall after it.
Compare
PatternMeaning
を皮切りにstarting with N, more follow
をもってas of N, it ends
Example sentences
  1. 東京とうきょう公演こうえんを皮切かわきりに、全国ぜんこくツアーが始はじまる。Tōkyō kōen o kawakiri ni, zenkoku tsuā ga hajimaru.Starting with the Tokyo show, a nationwide tour begins.
  2. 新商品しんしょうひんの発売はつばいを皮切かわきりに、各社かくしゃが次々つぎつぎと値下ねさげを行おこなった。Shinshōhin no hatsubai o kawakiri ni, kakusha ga tsugitsugi to nesage o okonatta.Starting with the new product's launch, each company cut prices one after another.
  3. 七月しちがつの大雨おおあめを皮切かわきりに、災害さいがいが相次あいついだ。Shichigatsu no ōame o kawakiri ni, saigai ga aitsuida.Starting with July's heavy rain, disasters followed one after another.
Common mistakes
  • 東京を皮切りに、公演は終わった。 → 東京を皮切りに、公演が続いた。
    皮切りに needs a chain of events to follow.
