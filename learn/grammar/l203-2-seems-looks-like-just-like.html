JLPT N3Unit 3

～みたいだ

Seems, looks like, just like

How to build it
Plain + みたいだN / なA + みたいだN + みたいに + V, N + みたいな + N
What it means

Use ～みたいだ in casual speech for a guess from what you see, or to compare one thing to another.

  • It's the casual form of ようだ.
  • It can guess from evidence or compare two things.
  • Nouns and なA join directly, with no な or の.
  • みたいに goes before verbs, みたいな before nouns.
Use ようだ in writing and みたいだ with friends.
Three uses
UseJapaneseMeaning
Guess外は雨みたいだ。It seems to be raining.
Comparisonあの子は人形みたいだ。That child looks like a doll.
Exampleカレーみたいなからいものが好きだ。I like spicy things such as curry.

Example sentences
  1. このスープ、少すこししおが足たりないみたいだ。Kono sūpu, sukoshi shio ga tarinai mitai da.This soup seems to need a little more salt.
  2. モチはお腹なかがすいているみたいだ。Mochi wa onaka ga suite iru mitai da.Mochi seems to be hungry.
  3. このケーキは雪ゆきみたいに白しろい。Kono kēki wa yuki mitai ni shiroi.This cake is as white as snow.
Common mistakes
  • 雨のみたいだ。 → 雨みたいだ。
    Nouns join directly.
  • 元気なみたいだ。 → 元気みたいだ。
    なA drop な.
