～の至り

The height of ...; extremely

How to build it
N + の至り
What it means

N + の至り expresses the extreme degree of a feeling, such as gratitude or embarrassment.

  • It is a fixed expression with a few nouns.
  • Common: 光栄の至り, 感激の至り, 若気の至り.
  • Used in speeches and formal talk.
Memorise the whole phrase: 光栄の至り, 感激の至り, 若気の至り.
Set phrases
PhraseMeaning
光栄の至りthe greatest honour
若気の至りyouthful folly
Example sentences
  1. 若気わかげの至いたりで、失礼しつれいなことを言いってしまいました。Wakage no itari de, shitsurei na koto o itte shimaimashita.In the excess of my youth, I said something rude.
  2. このようなお祝いわいをいただき、感激かんげきの至いたりです。Kono yō na oiwai o itadaki, kangeki no itari desu.I am overwhelmed with emotion at receiving such a celebration.
  3. 皆様みなさまの前まえで恥はをさらし、赤面せきめんの至いたりです。Minasama no mae de haji o sarashi, sekimen no itari desu.I am utterly embarrassed to have disgraced myself before you all.
Common mistakes
  • 元気の至りです。 → 光栄の至りです。
    Use only the set nouns that carry feeling.
