JLPT N1Unit 8

～にひきかえ

In contrast to ...

How to build it
N + にひきかえplain + のにひきかえ
What it means

N / plain form + にひきかえ contrasts two things, often with a feeling of judgement.

  • Two clearly different things are compared.
  • Both halves are facts, not wishes.
  • The speaker often shows surprise or criticism.
Use it when two things are surprisingly different.
Compare
PatternFeel
にひきかえstrong contrast
に対してneutral contrast
Example sentences
  1. 兄あにの成績せいせきにひきかえ、弟おとうとは勉強べんきょうが苦手にがてだ。Ani no seiseki ni hikikae, otōto wa benkyō ga nigate da.In contrast to my brother's grades, my younger brother is poor at study.
  2. 昨年さくねんの冷夏れいかにひきかえ、今年ことしは猛暑もうしょだ。Sakunen no reika ni hikikae, kotoshi wa mōsho da.In contrast to last year's cool summer, this year is blazing hot.
  3. 都会とかいの騒さわがしさにひきかえ、田舎いなかは静しずかだ。Tokai no sawagashisa ni hikikae, inaka wa shizuka da.In contrast to the noise of the city, the countryside is quiet.
Common mistakes
  • 兄にひきかえ、弟も優秀だ。 → 兄にひきかえ、弟は苦手だ。
    The two halves must contrast.
