JLPT N5Lesson 11

～だけ

Only

How to build it
N + だけQuantity + だけ + V
What it means

だけ attaches directly to a noun or a quantity to mean 'only': にちようびだけ, ひとりだけ. With a quantity it usually comes before the verb, just like the bare quantity: きってを いちまいだけ かいました. It sounds neutral, not negative.

  • Put だけ right after the thing or quantity you limit.
  • The verb stays positive. You don't need a negative.
  • It sounds neutral, not negative.
だけ in sentences
JapaneseMeaning
りんごを ひとつだけ たべました。I ate only one apple.
にちようびだけ やすみます。I only have Sunday off.
ひとりだけ います。There is only one person.
