JLPT N3Unit 7

～反面

But on the other hand (two sides of one thing)

How to build it
V / いA plain + 反面なA な + 反面N である + 反面
What it means

Use ～反面 to show that one thing has two opposite sides at the same time.

  • One subject shows two opposite sides.
  • Think pros and cons of a single thing.
  • It sounds formal. Speech often uses でも or けど.
Use it when writing about the good and bad of something.
Three kinds of contrast
PatternWhat it contrasts
反面Two sides of one thing
一方でTwo things, or two sides
に対してTwo different things
Example sentences
  1. この服ふくは安やすい反面はんめん、すぐに破やぶれる。Kono fuku wa yasui hanmen, sugu ni yabureru.These clothes are cheap, but they tear easily.
  2. ネットの買かい物ものは便利べんりな反面はんめん、失敗しっぱいも多おおい。Netto no kaimono wa benri na hanmen, shippai mo ōi.Online shopping is convenient, but it also leads to many mistakes.
  3. 給料きゅうりょうが上あがった反面はんめん、ぜいきんも高たかくなった。Kyūryō ga agatta hanmen, zeikin mo takaku natta.My pay went up, but on the other hand so did my taxes.
Common mistakes
  • 兄は背が高い反面、弟はひくい。 → 兄は背が高いのに対して、弟はひくい。
    反面 describes one subject, not two.
  • 便利の反面、高い。 → 便利な反面、高い。
    なA take な.
