S1 が、S2

S1, but S2

How to build it
S1 が、S2
What it means

が after a full sentence means 'but': it links two contrasting ideas. Put a comma after が. This is a different が from the subject marker you'll meet later.

  • が after a full sentence means but.
  • It links two contrasting ideas.
  • Put a comma after が.
  • This is a different が from the one after a noun.
Example sentences
  1. にほんの たべものは おいしいですが、たかいです。Nihon no tabemono wa oishii desu ga, takai desu.Japanese food is delicious, but expensive.
  2. しごとは いそがしいですが、おもしろいです。Shigoto wa isogashii desu ga, omoshiroi desu.Work is busy, but interesting.
