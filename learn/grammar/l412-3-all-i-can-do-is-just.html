JLPT N1Unit 12

～までだ

All I can do is ...; just ...

How to build it
V dictionary form + までだV た + までだ
What it means

V + までだ expresses either 'all there is left to do' or 'I only did ...'.

  • With the dictionary form: do it if nothing else works.
  • With the た-form: I only did it.
  • Both ways sound firm and slightly cool.
Read the tense: る means 'then I will', た means 'that's all I did'.
Two meanings
FormMeaning
V る + までだjust do it if need be
V た + までだI merely did
Example sentences
  1. 断ことわられたら、他ほかの店みせに行いくまでだ。Kotowarareta ra, hoka no mise ni iku made da.If I'm turned down, I'll just go to another shop.
  2. 私わたしは事実じじつを述のべたまでです。Watashi wa jijitsu o nobeta made desu.I merely stated the facts.
  3. 駄目だめでもともと、やるまでだ。Dame de mo tomo, yaru made da.Nothing to lose; I'll just do it.
