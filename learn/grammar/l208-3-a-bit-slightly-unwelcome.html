JLPT N3Unit 8

～気味

A bit, slightly (unwelcome)

How to build it
V ます-stem + 気味N + 気味気味だ / 気味で / 気味の + N
What it means

Use ～気味 to say you feel a slight touch of something, usually unpleasant.

  • It marks a small amount of a bad state.
  • It's read ぎみ here.
  • Common words: かぜ気味, 疲れ気味, 太り気味, 遅れ気味.
  • It doesn't fit good states.
かぜ気味 is the phrase you'll use most.
気味, がち and っぽい
WordMeaningExample
気味A bit, right now疲れ気味
がちOften happens忘れがち
っぽいLooks like, tends to忘れっぽい
Example sentences
  1. 最近さいきん太ふとり気味ぎみで、服ふくがきつくなった。Saikin futorigimi de, fuku ga kitsuku natta.I've been putting on a bit of weight lately, and my clothes have got tight.
  2. 今日きょうはかぜ気味ぎみなので、あたたかい服ふくを着きていく。Kyō wa kazegimi na node, atatakai fuku o kite iku.I feel like I'm getting a cold today, so I'll wear warm clothes.
  3. 疲つかれ気味ぎみのときは、明あかるい色いろの服ふくを着きる。Tsukaregimi no toki wa, akarui iro no fuku o kiru.When I'm a bit tired, I wear bright colours.
Common mistakes
  • 疲れる気味です。 → 疲れ気味です。
    Use the ます-stem.
  • 元気気味です。 → 少し元気です。
    It doesn't fit good states.
