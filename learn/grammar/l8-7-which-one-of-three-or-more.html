JLPT N5Lesson 8

どれ

Which one? (of three or more)

How to build it
N は どれですか
What it means

どれ means 'which one' when choosing among three or more things — it's the question word in the これ/それ/あれ family. Like これ, it stands alone and is not put directly before a noun. Answer by pointing it out, e.g. あの あおい かさです.

  • Use どれ when choosing among three or more things.
  • Like これ, it stands alone. No noun follows it.
  • Answer by pointing it out: あの あおい かさです.

Example sentences
  1. ベイカーさんの かさは どれですか。…あの あおい かさです。Beikā-san no kasa wa dore desu ka. …Ano aoi kasa desu.Which one is Mr. Baker's umbrella? — That blue one.
