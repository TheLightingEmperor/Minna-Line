ひとり・ふたり・～にん

Counting people

How to build it
N が + number + いますN が + なんにん + いますか
What it means

People are counted with ～にん, but one and two are irregular: ひとり and ふたり. Four people is よにん (not よんにん). Ask with なんにん. The pattern is N が + number + います: こどもが ふたり います can mean 'I have two children' or 'there are two children'.

  • Count people with ～にん, but one and two are special.
  • With people, います can mean there are or I have.
  • Use the short family words for your own family.
  • Use the polite family words for someone else's family.
ひとり and ふたり end in り. Everyone after that ends in にん.

Example sentences
  1. こどもが ふたり います。Kodomo ga futari imasu.I have two children.
  2. きょうだいが なんにん いますか。Kyōdai ga nannin imasu ka.How many siblings do you have?
  3. きょうしつに がくせいが ごにん います。Kyōshitsu ni gakusei ga gonin imasu.There are five students in the classroom.
Common mistakes
  • こどもが ににん います。 → こどもが ふたり います。
    Two people is ふたり, never ににん.
  • がくせいが よんにん います。 → がくせいが よにん います。
    Four people is よにん, not よんにん.
  • わたしの おにいさんは がくせいです。 → わたしの あには がくせいです。
    Your own brother is あに when talking to outsiders.
