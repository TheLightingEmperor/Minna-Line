N1 と N2

N1 and N2

How to build it
N1 と N2
What it means

と joins nouns: 'A と B' = 'A and B'. It lists all the items. It can only connect nouns — not verbs, adjectives or whole sentences.

  • と joins nouns and lists every item.
  • It cannot join verbs, adjectives (describing words) or whole sentences.
Example sentences
  1. ぎんこうの やすみは どようびと にちようびです。Ginkō no yasumi wa doyōbi to nichiyōbi desu.The bank is closed on Saturdays and Sundays.
  2. ベイカーさんと ロペスさんは かいしゃいんです。Beikā-san to Ropesu-san wa kaishain desu.Mr. Baker and Mr. Lopez are office workers.
