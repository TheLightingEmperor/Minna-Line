JLPT N2Unit 6

～か～ないかのうちに

Hardly... when

How to build it
V る / V た + か + V ない + か + のうちに
What it means

Use V る / V た + か + V ない + か + のうちに for two events so close in time that they almost overlap.

  • It describes two events almost at the same moment.
  • The verb appears twice: positive, then negative.
  • The second event is a quick reaction.
  • It is more vivid than とたん.
The first event has barely begun and the second already starts.
Pattern parts
PartRole
鳴ったかfirst event, in a hurry
鳴らないかthe first has barely happened
のうちにthen the second event
Example sentences
  1. ベルが鳴なったか鳴ならないかのうちに、生徒せいとたちは飛とび出だした。Beru ga natta ka naranai ka no uchi ni, seito-tachi wa tobidashita.The students dashed out almost as the bell rang.
  2. 布団ふとんに入はいるか入はいらないかのうちに、眠ねむってしまった。Futon ni hairu ka hairanai ka no uchi ni, nemutte shimatta.I fell asleep almost as soon as I got into bed.
  3. 電車でんしゃが止とまるか止とまらないかのうちに、客きゃくが降おり始はじめた。Densha ga tomaru ka tomaranai ka no uchi ni, kyaku ga ori hajimeta.Passengers began getting off almost before the train stopped.
Common mistakes
  • 鳴るか鳴るかのうちに → 鳴るか鳴らないかのうちに
    Use the positive then the negative of the same verb.
