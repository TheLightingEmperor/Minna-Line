～ため (written purpose and reason)

In order to, because of

How to build it
V dictionary + ため(に)plain clause + ためN + の + ため(に)
What it means

In writing, ため shows a purpose after a dictionary-form verb and a reason after a past or stative clause.

  • A dictionary-form action you control usually means purpose.
  • A past or stative clause means reason.
  • In writing, に is often dropped after ため.
  • ため is more formal than から and ので.
Look at the verb before ため: past tense almost always means a reason.
Purpose or reason?

Example sentences
  1. 工場こうじょうのきかいを新あたらしくするため、三日間みっかかん生産せいさんを止とめる。Kōjō no kikai o atarashiku suru tame, mikkakan seisan o tomeru.Production will stop for three days in order to replace the factory's machines.
  2. ざいりょうの値段ねだんが上あがったため、商品しょうひんも高たかくなった。Zairyō no nedan ga agatta tame, shōhin mo takaku natta.Because material prices rose, the products also became more expensive.
  3. 人ひとが足たりないため、工場こうじょうは夜よるは動うごいていない。Hito ga tarinai tame, kōjō wa yoru wa ugoite inai.The factory does not run at night because it is short of staff.
Common mistakes
  • 雪が降るため、トラックが遅れた。 → 雪が降ったため、トラックが遅れた。
    A finished cause takes the past form.
