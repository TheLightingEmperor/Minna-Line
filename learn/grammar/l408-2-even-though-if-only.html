JLPT N1Unit 8

～ものを

Even though / if only ...

How to build it
plain form + ものをV ば + plain past + ものを
What it means

Plain form + ものを expresses regret or complaint: things could have turned out well, but did not.

  • It ends the sentence with regret or complaint.
  • It implies the good result was possible.
  • It sometimes connects clauses with 'but'.
Imagine saying 'I wish you had ...' with a sigh.
Compare
PatternFeel
ものをregret, a pity
のにsimple contrast
Example sentences
  1. 早はやく言いってくれれば手伝てつだったものを。Hayaku itte kurereba tetsudatta mono o.If you had told me early, I would have helped!
  2. 素直すなおに謝あやまれば許ゆるしてもらえたものを、彼かれは黙だまっていた。Sunao ni ayamareba yurushite moraeta mono o, kare wa damatte ita.If he had apologised honestly he'd have been forgiven, yet he stayed silent.
  3. 行いかなければよかったものを、好奇心こうきしんに負まけた。Ikanakereba yokatta mono o, kōkishin ni maketa.He should not have gone, but curiosity got the better of him.
Common mistakes
  • 謝ればよかったものを、ありがとう。 → 謝ればよかったものを。
    ものを is a regret, so don't add thanks.
