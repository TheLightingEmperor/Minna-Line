JLPT N1Unit 10

～もさることながら

... of course, but also

How to build it
N1 + もさることながら + N2 + も
What it means

N1 もさることながら N2 means N1 is true, but N2 is even more so.

  • N1 is accepted, N2 is stressed.
  • Both parts are usually positive.
  • Use it in reviews and speeches.
Praise two things at once: 'A, of course, but B too'.
Compare
PatternFeel
もさることながらN1 yes, N2 even more
はもとよりN1 yes of course, N2 also
Example sentences
  1. 味あじもさることながら、見みた目めも素晴すばらしい。Aji mo saru koto nagara, mita me mo subarashii.The taste is wonderful, but the look is excellent too.
  2. 実力じつりょくもさることながら、運うんにも恵めぐまれた。Jitsuryoku mo saru koto nagara, un ni mo megumareta.His ability, of course, but luck also favoured him.
  3. 価格かかくもさることながら、品質ひんしつの高たかさが決きめ手てとなった。Kakaku mo saru koto nagara, hinshitsu no takasa ga kimete to natta.Price counts, of course, but the high quality was decisive.
Common mistakes
  • 味もさることながら、まずい。 → 味もさることながら、量も多い。
    N2 should be an equal or stronger point.
