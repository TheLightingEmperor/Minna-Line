こちら / そちら / あちら / どちら

Polite here, there, over there, where

How to build it
N は こちら / そちら / あちら ですN は どちらですか
What it means

こちら, そちら, あちら and どちら are the polite versions of ここ, そこ, あそこ and どこ, and also mean 'this way / that way'. Shop and hotel staff use them a lot. どちら is also the polite way to ask someone's country, company or school.

  • Shop and hotel staff use these a lot.
  • どちら also politely asks someone's country, company or school.
  • そちら can also mean your place or your side.
ここ vs こちら
EverydayPoliteMeaning
こここちらHere, this way
そこそちらThere, that way
あそこあちらOver there, that way
どこどちらWhere, which way
Example sentences
  1. エレベーターは あちらです。Erebētā wa achira desu.The elevator is that way. (polite)
  2. おくには どちらですか。…インドです。O-kuni wa dochira desu ka. …Indo desu.Where are you from? — India.
  3. かいしゃは どちらですか。…IMCです。Kaisha wa dochira desu ka. …IMC desu.Which company do you work for? — IMC.
