Causative with を and に

Marking the person who is made to act

How to build it
N1 は + N2 を + V causativeN1 は + N2 に + N3 を + V causative
What it means

The person made to act takes を with a verb that has no object, and に when the verb already has an object with を.

  • N1 gives the order and N2 does the action.
  • A sentence can't have two を for different roles.
  • If the verb has an object, the person takes に.
Count your を: there is only room for one.
を or に?
VerbPerson takesExample
いきますをこどもを いかせます
はしりますをこどもを はしらせます
よみますにこどもに ほんを よませます
たべますにこどもに やさいを たべさせます
そうじしますにこどもに へやを そうじさせます
Example sentences
  1. 父ちちは 弟おとうとを 買かい物ものに 行いかせました。Chichi wa otōto o kaimono ni ikasemashita.My father sent my younger brother shopping.
  2. 母ははは 弟おとうとに へやを そうじさせました。Haha wa otōto ni heya o sōji sasemashita.My mother made my younger brother clean his room.
  3. 駅長えきちょうは モチに きっぷを 売うらせました。Ekichō wa Mochi ni kippu o urasemashita.The stationmaster had Mochi sell tickets.
Common mistakes
  • こどもを ほんを よませます。 → こどもに ほんを よませます。
    The verb already has an object with を.
