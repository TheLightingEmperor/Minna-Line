S ね

…, isn't it? …, right?

How to build it
S ね
What it means

ね at the end of a sentence looks for agreement or shows sympathy, like '…isn't it?' or '…right?'. It is used when the speaker thinks the listener already knows or will agree. A very common reply is たいへんですね ('That must be hard').

  • ね looks for agreement or shows sympathy.
  • Use it when you think the listener knows or will agree.
  • A common reply is たいへんですね: That must be hard.
Example sentences
  1. まいばん じゅうにじまで べんきょうします。…たいへんですね。Maiban jūni-ji made benkyō shimasu. …Taihen desu ne.I study until 12 every night. — That must be hard.
  2. あしたは どようびですね。Ashita wa doyōbi desu ne.Tomorrow is Saturday, right?
