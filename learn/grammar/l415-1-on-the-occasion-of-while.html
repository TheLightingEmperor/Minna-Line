JLPT N1Unit 15

～がてら

On the occasion of ...; while

How to build it
V ます-stem + がてらN + がてら
What it means

V ます-stem / N + がてら means doing one thing while going out to do another.

  • One trip serves two purposes.
  • The second is a small errand.
  • The main verb is often 寄る or 行く.
Imagine killing two birds with one stone.
Compare
PatternFeel
がてらtwo purposes, one trip
かたがたformal, two purposes
Example sentences
  1. 散歩さんぽがてら、郵便局ゆうびんきょくに寄よった。Sanpo gatera, yūbinkyoku ni yotta.On my walk, I dropped by the post office.
  2. 買かい物ものがてら、友達ともだちの家いえに寄よった。Kaimono gatera, tomodachi no ie ni yotta.While shopping, I stopped by a friend's house.
  3. 旅行りょこうがてら、実家じっかに顔かおを出だす。Ryokō gatera, jikka ni kao o dasu.I'll visit my parents' home on my trip.
Common mistakes
  • 家で勉強がてら、買い物した。 → 散歩がてら、買い物した。
    がてら needs a movement.
