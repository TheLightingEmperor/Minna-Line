JLPT N2Unit 5

～かねない

May well; could end up

How to build it
V ます-stem + かねない
What it means

Use V ます-stem + かねない to warn that something bad could easily happen.

  • It means it could well happen, with a bad outcome.
  • The speaker is warning, not hoping.
  • It is stronger than かもしれない.
  • It is often written or formal.
A warning phrase: if you go on like this, it might happen.
かねない or かねる
PatternMeaning
なりかねないmay well become
お受けしかねるcannot accept
Example sentences
  1. そんなに飲のむと、体からだを壊こわしかねない。Sonna ni nomu to, karada o kowashikanenai.If you drink that much, you could ruin your health.
  2. スピードを出だしすぎると、事故じこを起おこしかねない。Supīdo o dashisugiru to, jiko o okoshikanenai.If you speed, you could cause an accident.
  3. 彼かれならそんなことも言いいかねない。Kare nara sonna koto mo iikanenai.He is the kind who might even say such a thing.
Common mistakes
  • 優勝しかねない。 → 優勝するかもしれない。
    かねない is for bad outcomes only.
