～てしかるべき

Should be ...; ought to

How to build it
V て + しかるべきだしかるべき + N
What it means

V て / N + しかるべき says that something is natural and proper, and should have happened.

  • Something is natural but not yet done.
  • It has a mild critical edge.
  • しかるべき + N means 'proper N'.
Pair it with passive forms: 評価されてしかるべき.
Forms
FormMeaning
て + しかるべきshould be done
しかるべき Nproper N
Example sentences
  1. この件けんについては、もっと議論ぎろんされてしかるべきだ。Kono ken ni tsuite wa, motto giron sarete shikarubeki da.This matter ought to be discussed much more.
  2. 彼かれはもっと評価ひょうかされてしかるべき人物じんぶつだ。Kare wa motto hyōka sarete shikarubeki jinbutsu da.He is a person who deserves much more credit.
  3. 責任者せきにんしゃには、しかるべき処分しょぶんが下くだされるだろう。Sekininsha ni wa, shikarubeki shobun ga kudasareru darō.A proper punishment will be handed to the person responsible.
