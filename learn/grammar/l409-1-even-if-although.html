JLPT N1Unit 9

～といえども

Even if ...; although

How to build it
N + といえどもplain + といえども
What it means

N / clause + といえども admits a fact but adds that the rule or expectation still applies.

  • Even an exceptional case follows the rule.
  • The second half is a firm statement.
  • It is a written, stiff style.
Often used with high rank: 社長, 専門家, 天才.
Compare
PatternFeel
といえどもwritten, firm
とはいえadmit, then contrast
Example sentences
  1. 社長しゃちょうといえども、法律ほうりつを守まもらなければならない。Shachō to iedomo, hōritsu o mamoranakereba naranai.Even a company president must obey the law.
  2. 子供こどもといえども、責任せきにんは免まぬかれない。Kodomo to iedomo, sekinin wa manukarenai.Even a child cannot escape responsibility.
  3. 一日いちにちといえども、休やすむことはできない。Ichinichi to iedomo, yasumu koto wa dekinai.Even for a single day, we cannot rest.
