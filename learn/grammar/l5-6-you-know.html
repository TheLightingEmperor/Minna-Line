S よ

…, you know

How to build it
S よ
What it means

よ at the end of a sentence tells the listener something they don't know, or stresses your own judgement: '…, you know'. Compare ね, which you use when you think the listener already knows. Using よ too much can sound pushy.

  • よ can also stress your own opinion.
  • Using よ too much can sound pushy.
ね vs よ
EndingUse it whenExampleEnglish
ねThe listener already knowsあしたは どようびですね。Tomorrow is Saturday, right?
よThe listener doesn't knowあしたは やすみですよ。Tomorrow is a day off, you know.
Example sentences
  1. この でんしゃは こうしえんへ いきますか。…いいえ、いきません。つぎの「ふつう」ですよ。Kono densha wa Kōshien e ikimasu ka. …Iie, ikimasen. Tsugi no 'futsū' desu yo.Does this train go to Koshien? — No, it doesn't. It's the next local train, you know.
  2. あしたは やすみですよ。Ashita wa yasumi desu yo.Tomorrow is a day off, you know.
