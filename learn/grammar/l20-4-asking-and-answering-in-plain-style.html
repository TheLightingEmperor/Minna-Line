Casual questions and うん / ううん

Asking and answering in plain style

How to build it
Plain form + ？な-adjective / N + ？
What it means

In casual speech, questions usually drop か and just rise in intonation: コーヒー、のむ？ With nouns and な-adjectives, だ is also dropped in questions: ひま？ (not ひまだ？). Particles like を and へ are often left out. Answer 'yes' with うん and 'no' with ううん.

  • Casual questions usually drop か.
  • Your voice rises at the end instead.
  • With nouns and な-adjectives, だ drops too.
  • Particles like を and へ are often left out.

Example sentences
  1. コーヒー、のむ？ …うん、のむ。Kōhī, nomu? … Un, nomu.Want some coffee? — Yeah, I'll have some.
  2. あした ひま？ …ううん、ひまじゃ ない。Ashita hima? … Uun, hima ja nai.Free tomorrow? — No, I'm not.
Common mistakes
  • あした ひまだ？ → あした ひま？
    だ drops in casual questions.
