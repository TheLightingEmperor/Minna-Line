JLPT N1Unit 20

～ともなく

Without really ...ing

How to build it
V dictionary form + ともなくQ-word + ともなく
What it means

V dictionary form + ともなく expresses doing an action vaguely or without a clear intention.

  • The action is done almost without thinking.
  • Typical verbs: 見る, 聞く, 言う.
  • Q-word forms: どこからともなく, 誰ともなく.
Remember どこからともなく ('from out of nowhere').
Q-word forms
PhraseMeaning
どこからともなくfrom somewhere
誰ともなくsomeone, anyone
Example sentences
  1. 彼かれは誰だれに言いうともなく、つぶやいた。Kare wa dare ni iu to mo naku, tsubuyaita.He murmured, not really to anyone.
  2. 見みるともなく窓まどの外そとを見みていた。Miru to mo naku mado no soto o mite ita.I was looking out the window without really looking.
  3. 聞きくともなく、隣となりの話はなしが耳みみに入はいった。Kiku to mo naku, tonari no hanashi ga mimi ni haitta.Without really listening, I overheard the neighbours' talk.
