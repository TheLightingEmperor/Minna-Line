～に至るまで

Right down to ...; all the way to ...

How to build it
N + に至るまでA から B に至るまで
What it means

N + に至るまで shows a wide range that reaches even the far or small end.

  • It stresses that even the farthest item is included.
  • It often pairs with から to show a range.
  • It is more formal than まで.
Use it when you want to say 'everything, even the very last bit'.
Compare
PatternFeel
までplain: up to
に至るまでstronger: even down to
Example sentences
  1. この寺てらは建物たてものから庭にわの石いしに至いたるまで、江戸えど時代じだいのままだ。Kono tera wa tatemono kara niwa no ishi ni itaru made, Edo jidai no mama da.This temple is as it was in the Edo period, right down to the stones of the garden.
  2. 祭まつりは朝あさから晩ばんに至いたるまで、町まちじゅうが賑にぎわった。Matsuri wa asa kara ban ni itaru made, machijū ga nigiwatta.From morning until night, the whole town was lively at the festival.
  3. 社長しゃちょうから新入社員しんにゅうしゃいんに至いたるまで、みんなが参加さんかした。Shachō kara shinnyū shain ni itaru made, minna ga sanka shita.Everyone took part, from the president down to the new employees.
