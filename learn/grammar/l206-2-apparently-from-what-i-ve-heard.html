～らしい (I hear, it seems)

Apparently, from what I've heard

How to build it
V / いA plain + らしいなA / N + らしい
What it means

Use ～らしい to pass on what you heard or read, without taking responsibility for it.

  • The information comes from outside, not your own view.
  • You keep a step away from the claim.
  • Nouns and なA drop だ.
  • It conjugates like an いA: らしかった, らしくて.
Use it for rumours and second-hand news.

Example sentences
  1. 駅前えきまえに新あたらしいビルができるらしい。Ekimae ni atarashii biru ga dekiru rashii.Apparently a new building is going up in front of the station.
  2. あの店みせは先月せんげつ閉店へいてんしたらしいよ。Ano mise wa sengetsu heiten shita rashii yo.I heard that shop closed last month.
  3. この町まちは夜よるは静しずからしい。Kono machi wa yoru wa shizuka rashii.This town is apparently quiet at night.
Common mistakes
  • あの店は休みだらしい。 → あの店は休みらしい。
    Drop だ before らしい.
  • 私は来年けっこんするらしい。 → 私は来年けっこんするつもりだ。
    Don't use らしい for your own plans.
