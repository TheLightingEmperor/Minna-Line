～くせに

Even though (criticising someone)

How to build it
V / いA plain + くせになA な + くせにN の + くせに
What it means

Use ～くせに to criticise a person whose behaviour doesn't match what you would expect of them.

  • It shows blame, scorn or annoyance.
  • Both halves need the same person as subject.
  • Don't use it about yourself.
  • It's too strong for superiors or polite talk.
Say it only with people close to you, because it stings.
のに and くせに
PatternFeelingSubject
のにSurprise, disappointmentCan differ
くせにBlame, scornSame person, not you
Example sentences
  1. 兄あには知しっているくせに、教おしえてくれない。Ani wa shitte iru kuse ni, oshiete kurenai.My brother knows, but he won't tell me.
  2. 彼かれは若わかいくせに、すぐ疲つかれたと言いう。Kare wa wakai kuse ni, sugu tsukareta to iu.He's young, yet he's always quick to say he's tired.
  3. 親おやのくせに、子こどものたんじょう日びを忘わすれたの？Oya no kuse ni, kodomo no tanjōbi o wasureta no?You're a parent, and you forgot your child's birthday?
Common mistakes
  • 私は学生のくせに、勉強しない。 → 私は学生なのに、勉強しない。
    Don't use くせに about yourself.
  • 春のくせに、寒い。 → 春なのに、寒い。
    くせに needs a person as subject.
