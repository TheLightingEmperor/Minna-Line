N + なら

If it's that you're talking about

How to build it
N + なら、～V plain + なら、～
What it means

なら picks up what the other person has just raised and adds advice or information about it.

  • It responds to something just mentioned.
  • The second half gives advice, an opinion or information.
  • It sounds like if it's that you mean.
  • Nouns join directly, without だ.
It's the word for good recommendations: X なら, Y が いいですよ.
A short exchange
SpeakerJapaneseMeaning
Aおんせんに いきたいんですが。I'd like to go to a hot spring.
Bおんせんなら、はこねが いいですよ。For hot springs, Hakone is good.

Word typeJapaneseMeaning

Example sentences
  1. カメラなら、あの 店みせが 安やすいですよ。Kamera nara, ano mise ga yasui desu yo.If it's a camera you want, that shop is cheap.
  2. モチに 会あいたいんですか。モチなら 駅えきに いますよ。Mochi ni aitai n desu ka. Mochi nara eki ni imasu yo.You want to see Mochi? Mochi is at the station.
  3. 海うみへ 行いくなら、夏なつが いいですよ。Umi e iku nara, natsu ga ii desu yo.If you're going to the sea, summer is good.
Common mistakes
  • カメラだなら、あの みせが いいです。 → カメラなら、あの みせが いいです。
    Don't put だ before なら.
  • たかいだなら、かいません。 → たかいなら、かいません。
    い-adjectives join なら directly.
