JLPT N1Unit 18

～にして

Only at ...; both ... and

How to build it
N + にしてN1 + にして + N2
What it means

N + にして marks the point at which a rare thing is possible, or joins two properties.

  • With ages it means 'at the very age of'.
  • Between two nouns it means 'both'.
  • A level of surprise is implied.
Think of it as 'already at ...' or 'is both ... and'.
Two uses
UseExample
age十歳にして
both学者にして政治家
Example sentences
  1. 彼かれは十歳じっさいにして大学だいがくに入はいった。Kare wa jissai ni shite daigaku ni haitta.He entered university at the age of ten.
  2. 三十年さんじゅうねんの歳月さいげつを経へて、今いまにして分わかる。Sanjūnen no saigetsu o hete, ima ni shite wakaru.After thirty years, only now do I understand.
  3. 彼かれは学者がくしゃにして政治家せいじかでもある。Kare wa gakusha ni shite seijika de mo aru.He is both a scholar and a politician.
