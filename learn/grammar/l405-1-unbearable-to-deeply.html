JLPT N1Unit 5

～に堪えない

Unbearable to ...; deeply ...

How to build it
V dictionary form + に堪えないN + に堪えない
What it means

V / N + に堪えない means either too awful to bear, or a very deep feeling.

  • With 見る and 聞く it means unbearable.
  • With feeling nouns like 感謝 it means very deep.
  • It is formal and often critical.
Pair it with 堪える (to endure): it cannot be endured.
Two meanings
UseExample
unbearable見るに堪えない
deeply felt感謝に堪えない
Example sentences
  1. その映像えいぞうは見みるに堪たえないほどひどかった。Sono eizō wa miru ni taenai hodo hidokatta.The footage was so terrible I could hardly look at it.
  2. 彼かれの嘘うそは聞きくに堪たえない。Kare no uso wa kiku ni taenai.His lies are unbearable to listen to.
  3. 御礼おれいの言葉ことばもなく、感謝かんしゃに堪たえません。Orei no kotoba mo naku, kansha ni taemasen.I have no words of thanks; my gratitude is beyond measure.
Common mistakes
  • 美しくて見るに堪えない。 → ひどくて見るに堪えない。
    堪えない is negative; do not use it for beauty.
