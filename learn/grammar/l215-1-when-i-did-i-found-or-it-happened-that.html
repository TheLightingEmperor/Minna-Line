JLPT N3Unit 15

～たら、～た

When I did ~, I found or it happened that ~

How to build it
V た + ら、clause (past)
What it means

With a past ending, たら reports something you found or something that happened after an action.

  • The second half is past tense.
  • The result is outside your control.
  • Often it comes as a surprise.
  • For your own planned next step, use てから.
If you can add 'and to my surprise' in English, たら～た probably fits.
Same たら, different ending
JapaneseMeaning
家に帰ったら、電話しますI'll call when I get home
家に帰ったら、猫がいたWhen I got home, there was a cat
Example sentences
  1. 窓まどを開あけたら、雪ゆきが降ふっていた。Mado o aketara, yuki ga futte ita.When I opened the window, it was snowing.
  2. 朝あさ起おきたら、庭にわが真まっ白しろだった。Asa okitara, niwa ga masshiro datta.When I woke up, the garden was pure white.
  3. 山やまに登のぼったら、急きゅうに天気てんきが悪わるくなった。Yama ni nobottara, kyū ni tenki ga waruku natta.When I climbed the mountain, the weather suddenly turned bad.
Common mistakes
  • うちに帰ったら、宿題をした。 → うちに帰ってから、宿題をした。
    The result must be outside your control. Your own planned act takes てから.
