JLPT N3Unit 21

～ほど～ない・～ほど～はない

Not as ~ as; nothing is as ~ as

How to build it
A は B ほど + negativeN ほど + adj + N はない
What it means

AはBほど～ない says A falls short of B, and Nほど～はない says N is the most.

  • The ending must be negative.
  • The thing after ほど is the stronger one.
  • ほど～はない praises something as the most.
  • くらい～はない means the same.
Use ～ほど～はない to praise. It is warmer than いちばん.
Comparison patterns
JapaneseMeaning
今日は昨日より寒いtoday is colder than yesterday
今日は昨日ほど寒くないtoday isn't as cold as yesterday
今日ほど寒い日はないno day is as cold as today
Example sentences
  1. 今日きょうは昨日きのうほどじめじめしていない。Kyō wa kinō hodo jimejime shite inai.Today isn't as muggy as yesterday.
  2. うちの猫ねこほどのんびりした猫ねこはいない。Uchi no neko hodo nonbiri shita neko wa inai.There's no cat as laid-back as ours.
  3. 寒さむい朝あさのふとんほど、ぬくぬくしたものはない。Samui asa no futon hodo, nukunuku shita mono wa nai.Nothing is as snug as a futon on a cold morning.
Common mistakes
  • 今日は昨日ほど寒い。 → 今日は昨日ほど寒くない。
    A comparison with ほど needs a negative ending.
