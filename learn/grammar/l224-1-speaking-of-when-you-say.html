JLPT N3Unit 24

～といえば・～というと

Speaking of; when you say

How to build it
N + といえばN + というと
What it means

といえば and というと pick up a topic and say the first thing it brings to mind.

  • といえば often shifts the conversation.
  • Both can give the typical example of something.
  • というと？ asks 'you mean?'.
  • They do not define a word.
Use といえば to steer a chat toward what you want to talk about.
Topic or definition
JapaneseMeaning
京都といえば秋だKyoto makes me think of autumn
京都というと秋を思い出すKyoto reminds me of autumn
学割というのは割引だgakuwari means a discount
Example sentences
  1. 日本にほんの夏なつといえば、花火はなびだ。Nihon no natsu to ieba, hanabi da.When you think of summer in Japan, it's fireworks.
  2. スーパーといえば、駅前えきまえに新あたらしい店みせができたよ。Sūpā to ieba, ekimae ni atarashii mise ga dekita yo.Speaking of supermarkets, a new one has opened by the station.
  3. ハワイというと、青あおい海うみを思おもい出だす。Hawai to iu to, aoi umi o omoidasu.When I hear Hawaii, I think of the blue sea.
Common mistakes
  • 学割といえば、学生の割引のことです。 → 学割というのは、学生の割引のことです。
    For a definition, use というのは.
