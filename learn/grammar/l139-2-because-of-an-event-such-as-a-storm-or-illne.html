N で (cause)

Because of an event, such as a storm or illness

How to build it
N で + result
What it means

で after an event noun such as じしん, たいふう, じこ or びょうき gives the cause of what happened.

  • N is an event or a condition, not a person.
  • The second half is something that happened or a state.
  • Requests and plans can't follow this で.
Read this で as 'because of' whenever an event noun comes before it.
Nouns that often take this で
JapaneseMeaning
じしんearthquake
たいふうtyphoon
かじfire
じこaccident
びょうきillness
かぜa cold
ゆきsnow
あめrain
Example sentences
  1. 台風たいふうで 電車でんしゃが 止とまりました。Taifū de densha ga tomarimashita.The trains stopped because of the typhoon.
  2. モチは かぜで 今日きょうの 仕事しごとを 休やすみました。Mochi wa kaze de kyō no shigoto o yasumimashita.Mochi took today off work because of a cold.
  3. 火事かじで 古ふるい 建物たてものが なくなりました。Kaji de furui tatemono ga nakunarimashita.The old building was lost in the fire.
Common mistakes
  • たいふうで、いえに いて ください。 → たいふうなので、いえに いて ください。
    A request can't follow で. Use ので.
  • じしんに ビルが たおれました。 → じしんで ビルが たおれました。
    A cause takes で, not に.
