JLPT N1Unit 24

～だに

Even ...; the mere thought

How to build it
V dictionary form + だにN + だに + negative
What it means

V dictionary form / N + だに shows that even the smallest case is so; with 想像する it means even the thought is frightening.

  • It points to the smallest case.
  • With 想像する, 考える it expresses fear or disgust.
  • With a negative it means 'not even'.
Treat 想像するだに恐ろしい as a stock phrase.
Fixed phrases
PhraseMeaning
想像するだにeven to imagine
夢にだにnot even in a dream
Example sentences
  1. 想像そうぞうするだに恐おそろしい。Sōzō suru dani osoroshii.Even to imagine it is frightening.
  2. 彼かれは夢ゆめにだに思おもわなかった。Kare wa yume ni dani omowanakatta.He never even dreamed of it.
  3. あの事件じけんは考かんがえるだに身震みぶるいがする。Ano jiken wa kangaeru dani miburui ga suru.Just thinking about that incident makes me shudder.
