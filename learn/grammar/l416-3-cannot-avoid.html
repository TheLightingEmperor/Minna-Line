JLPT N1Unit 16

～を免れない

Cannot avoid ...

How to build it
N + を免れない
What it means

N + を免れない means one cannot escape a bad result, such as criticism or blame.

  • It says a bad result is unavoidable.
  • Nouns: 批判, 責任, 非難, 失敗.
  • It is a firm, written expression.
Link it with 避けられない (unavoidable).
Typical nouns
NounMeaning
責任responsibility
批判criticism
Example sentences
  1. 今回こんかいの失敗しっぱいは、批判ひはんを免まぬかれない。Konkai no shippai wa, hihan o manukarenai.This failure cannot escape criticism.
  2. 経営者けいえいしゃは責任せきにんを免まぬかれない。Keieisha wa sekinin o manukarenai.The managers cannot escape responsibility.
  3. この計画けいかくは、失敗しっぱいを免まぬかれないだろう。Kono keikaku wa, shippai o manukarenai darō.This plan will not avoid failure.
Common mistakes
  • 成功を免れない。 → 批判を免れない。
    Only negative nouns fit を免れない.
