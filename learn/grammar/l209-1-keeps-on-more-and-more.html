JLPT N3Unit 9

～一方だ

Keeps on (more and more)

How to build it
V る + 一方だ
What it means

Use V る + 一方だ to say a change keeps going in one direction, often a bad one.

  • It needs a verb of change.
  • The change goes one way, with no sign of stopping.
  • It's usually a bad trend.
Picture a graph line that never turns back.
Common partners
JapaneseMeaning
増える一方だKeeps increasing
へる一方だKeeps decreasing
上がる一方だKeeps rising
悪くなる一方だKeeps getting worse
Example sentences
  1. この町まちの人口じんこうはへる一方いっぽうだ。Kono machi no jinkō wa heru ippō da.This town's population just keeps falling.
  2. 物ものの値段ねだんは上あがる一方いっぽうだ。Mono no nedan wa agaru ippō da.Prices just keep going up.
  3. 仕事しごとが増ふえる一方いっぽうで、休やすみが取とれない。Shigoto ga fueru ippō de, yasumi ga torenai.My work just keeps increasing, so I can't take time off.
Common mistakes
  • 毎日勉強する一方だ。 → 毎日勉強している。
    It needs a verb of change.
  • 増えた一方だ。 → 増える一方だ。
    Use the dictionary form.
