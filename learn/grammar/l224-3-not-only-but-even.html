JLPT N3Unit 24

～ばかりか

Not only ~ but even

How to build it
N + ばかりかV・い-adj plain + ばかりかな-adj な + ばかりか
What it means

ばかりか is a stronger 'not only', and the second half adds something more surprising.

  • The second half is more surprising.
  • Often paired with も, まで or さえ.
  • The ending cannot be a command or request.
  • It is more written than だけでなく.
Use it when your eyebrows go up as you say the second half.
だけでなく or ばかりか
JapaneseFeeling
英語だけでなく中国語も話すneutral addition
英語ばかりか中国語まで話すsurprise, even more than expected
Example sentences
  1. 彼かれは遅おくれて来きたばかりか、宿題しゅくだいも忘わすれた。Kare wa okurete kita bakari ka, shukudai mo wasureta.Not only did he come late, he forgot his homework too.
  2. この漢字かんじは子供こどもばかりか、大人おとなも読よめない。Kono kanji wa kodomo bakari ka, otona mo yomenai.Not only children but even adults can't read this kanji.
  3. 彼女かのじょは英語えいごばかりか、アラビア語ごまで話はなせる。Kanojo wa eigo bakari ka, Arabia-go made hanaseru.She speaks not only English but even Arabic.
Common mistakes
  • 英語ばかりか、中国語も話してください。 → 英語だけでなく、中国語も話してください。
    A request cannot follow ばかりか.
