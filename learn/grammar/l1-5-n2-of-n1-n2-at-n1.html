N1 の N2 (belonging to)

N2 of N1, N2 at N1

How to build it
N1 の N2
What it means

の joins two nouns; the first noun describes the second. Here N1 is the group the person belongs to: a company, school or hospital. Note the order is the reverse of English 'of': IMCの しゃいん = 'employee of IMC'.

  • の joins two nouns. The first one describes the second.
  • Here N1 is the group: a company, school or hospital.
  • The order is the reverse of English of.
Example sentences
  1. ミラーさんは IMCの しゃいんです。Mirā-san wa IMC no shain desu.Mr. Miller is an IMC employee.
  2. カリナさんは ふじだいがくの がくせいです。Karina-san wa Fuji-daigaku no gakusei desu.Ms. Karina is a student at Fuji University.
  3. ワンさんは こうべびょういんの いしゃです。Wan-san wa Kōbe-byōin no isha desu.Mr. Wang is a doctor at Kobe Hospital.
Common mistakes
  • カリナさんは がくせいの ふじだいがくです。 → カリナさんは ふじだいがくの がくせいです。
    The group comes first, then the role.
