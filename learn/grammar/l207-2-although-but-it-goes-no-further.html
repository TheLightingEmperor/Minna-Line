～ものの

Although (but it goes no further)

How to build it
V / いA plain + もののなA な + もののN である + ものの
What it means

Use ～ものの to admit a fact and then say that what follows doesn't go as expected.

  • The first half is true, but the second falls short.
  • It's slightly formal and common in writing.
  • V た + ものの fits plans that stalled.
  • The second half can't be a request or plan.
The set phrase とはいうものの means that said.
Saying although
PatternFeeling
のにSurprise or complaint
けれどもNeutral but
もののI admit it, but things stalled
Example sentences
  1. 新あたらしいパソコンを買かったものの、使つかい方かたがわからない。Atarashii pasokon o katta mono no, tsukaikata ga wakaranai.I bought a new computer, but I don't know how to use it.
  2. セールに行いったものの、ほしい物ものはなかった。Sēru ni itta mono no, hoshii mono wa nakatta.I went to the sale, but there was nothing I wanted.
  3. お金かねはあるものの、使つかう時間じかんがない。O-kane wa aru mono no, tsukau jikan ga nai.I have money, but no time to spend it.
Common mistakes
  • 高いものの、買ってください。 → 高いけれど、買ってください。
    No requests after ものの.
  • 便利のものの、高い。 → 便利なものの、高い。
    なA take な.
