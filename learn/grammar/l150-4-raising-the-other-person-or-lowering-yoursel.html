そんけいご or けんじょうご?

Raising the other person or lowering yourself

How to build it
Their action: そんけいごMy action: けんじょうご
What it means

Use そんけいご for what the other person does and けんじょうご for what you or your own group does, and treat your own company as your side with outsiders.

  • First ask who is doing the action.
  • Your own company counts as your side with outsiders.
  • Drop さん from your own colleagues when talking to outsiders.
One question solves most cases: who is doing this, them or me?

Example sentences
  1. 先生せんせいは 明日あした いらっしゃいますか。Sensei wa ashita irasshaimasu ka.Will the teacher be coming tomorrow?
  2. はい、私わたしも まいります。Hai, watashi mo mairimasu.Yes, I will come too.
  3. 社長しゃちょうの やまだは 今いま 会社かいしゃに おりません。Shachō no Yamada wa ima kaisha ni orimasen.Our president, Yamada, isn't in the office right now.
Common mistakes
  • せんせいは コーヒーを いただきます。 → せんせいは コーヒーを めしあがります。
    いただきます is humble. Don't use it for your teacher.
  • わたしは あした いらっしゃいます。 → わたしは あした まいります。
    Never raise yourself.
  • やまだぶちょうは いらっしゃいません。 (to a customer) → やまだは おりません。
    Your boss is on your side with outsiders.
