N1 から N2 まで

From N1 to N2

How to build it
N1 から N2 までN は N1 から N2 までです
What it means

から means 'from' and まで means 'until / up to', for both times and places. They can be used together or alone, and you can end with です: 'ぎんこうは くじから さんじまでです' (the bank is open 9 to 3).

  • から means from. まで means until or up to.
  • They work for both times and places.
  • You can use them together or alone.
  • You can end the sentence with です.
Example sentences
  1. ぎんこうは くじから さんじまでです。Ginkō wa ku-ji kara san-ji made desu.The bank is open from 9 to 3.
  2. まいにち くじから ごじまで はたらきます。Mainichi ku-ji kara go-ji made hatarakimasu.I work from 9 to 5 every day.
  3. ひるやすみは じゅうにじから いちじまでです。Hiruyasumi wa jūni-ji kara ichi-ji made desu.Lunch break is from 12 to 1.
