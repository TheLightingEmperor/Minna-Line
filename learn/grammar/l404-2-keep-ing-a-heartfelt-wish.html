～てやまない

Keep ...ing (a heartfelt wish)

How to build it
V て + やまないV て + やみません
What it means

V て + やまない expresses a feeling that keeps going without stopping, such as hope, love or respect.

  • The feeling continues for a long time.
  • Used for wishes, love, respect and hope.
  • It is formal, in speeches and letters.
Use it at the end of a letter or speech for heartfelt feelings.
Typical verbs
VerbMeaning
願うwish
尊敬するrespect
Example sentences
  1. 皆様みなさまのご健康けんこうを祈いのってやみません。Minasama no go-kenkō o inotte yamimasen.I keep praying for everyone's good health.
  2. 彼かれは私わたしが尊敬そんけいしてやまない恩師おんしだ。Kare wa watashi ga sonkei shite yamanai onshi da.He is a mentor I respect without end.
  3. 故郷こきょうの山やまは、今いまも愛あいしてやまない場所ばしょだ。Kokyō no yama wa, ima mo ai shite yamanai basho da.The mountains of my hometown are a place I never stop loving.
Common mistakes
  • 走ってやまない。 → 成功を信じてやまない。
    やまない is for emotional verbs, not physical action.
