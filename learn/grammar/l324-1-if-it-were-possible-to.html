JLPT N2Unit 24

～ものなら

If (it were possible to)

How to build it
V potential + ものなら
What it means

Use V potential + ものなら for a wish that is nearly impossible, or as a challenge or warning.

  • It describes a wish that is nearly out of reach.
  • The verb is usually in the potential form.
  • The second half is a wish: たい or ほしい.
  • It can also be a challenge: やれるものなら。
A wish that's almost out of reach.
Two uses
JapaneseMeaning
戻れるものなら戻りたいwish, nearly impossible
できるものならやってみろchallenge to another
Example sentences
  1. できるものなら、もう一度いちど若わかい頃ころに戻もどりたい。Dekiru mono nara, mō ichido wakai koro ni modoritai.If I could, I'd like to go back to my youth once more.
  2. 行いけるものなら、今いますぐ行いきたい。Ikeru mono nara, ima sugu ikitai.If I could go, I'd go right now.
  3. やれるものなら、やってみろ。Yareru mono nara, yatte miro.If you think you can, go ahead and try.
Common mistakes
  • 行くものなら行きたい。 → 行けるものなら行きたい。
    Use the potential form for the wish.
