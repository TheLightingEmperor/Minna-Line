V dictionary / ない + ように

So that something happens, or doesn't

How to build it
V potential + ように、～V ない + ように、～
What it means

ように gives an aim, and the verb before it is usually a potential, a verb like わかります, or a ない form.

  • The first half is the aim. The second half is what you do.
  • The aim is something you can't fully control.
  • That means potentials, ない forms, and verbs like わかります.
  • The second half is an action you choose to do.
Ask yourself: is this something I hope will happen? If so, use ように.

Example sentences
  1. 電車でんしゃに おくれないように、早はやく 家いえを 出でます。Densha ni okurenai yō ni, hayaku ie o demasu.I leave home early so that I'm not late for the train.
  2. 後うしろの 人ひとにも 聞きこえるように、大おおきい こえで 話はなします。Ushiro no hito ni mo kikoeru yō ni, ōkii koe de hanashimasu.I speak in a loud voice so that people at the back can hear too.
  3. 朝あさ 起おきられるように、今夜こんやは 早はやく ねます。Asa okirareru yō ni, kon'ya wa hayaku nemasu.I'll go to bed early tonight so that I can get up in the morning.
Common mistakes
  • わすれますように、メモします。 → わすれないように、メモします。
    ように joins plain forms, and here the aim is not forgetting.
  • およぐように、れんしゅうします。 → およげるように、れんしゅうします。
    The aim is being able to swim, so use the potential.
