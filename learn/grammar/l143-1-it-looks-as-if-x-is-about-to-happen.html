V ます-stem + そうです

It looks as if X is about to happen

How to build it
V ます-stem + そうです
What it means

V ます-stem + そうです says that from what you can see, something looks about to happen.

  • It is based on what you can see now.
  • Remove ます, then add そうです.
  • Good for warnings and quick reactions.
Say it out loud when you see something about to go wrong: おちそう！
Making the そう form

Example sentences
  1. モチが やねから おちそうです。Mochi ga yane kara ochisō desu.Mochi looks about to fall off the roof.
  2. 風かぜで 木きが たおれそうです。Kaze de ki ga taoresō desu.The tree looks as if the wind will blow it over.
  3. 電車でんしゃが もう 出でそうです。いそぎましょう。Densha ga mō desō desu. Isogimashō.The train looks about to leave. Let's hurry.
Common mistakes
  • あめが ふるそうです。 (looks like rain) → あめが ふりそうです。
    ふるそうです means 'I heard it will rain'.
  • おちますそうです。 → おちそうです。
    Remove ます first.
