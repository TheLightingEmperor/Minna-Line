なん vs なに

Two readings of what

How to build it
なん + です / の / counterなに + を / が
What it means

Both mean 'what'. Use なん before sounds t, d and n (なんですか, なんの) and before counters (なんじ, なんさい, なんがい). Use なに before most particles, such as を and が: なにを たべますか.

  • Both mean what.
  • Use なん when asking about a number or category.
Before t, d or n sounds and counters, say なん. Before most particles, say なに.

Example sentences
  1. それは なんですか。Sore wa nan desu ka.What is that?
  2. なにを のみますか。Nani o nomimasu ka.What will you drink?
