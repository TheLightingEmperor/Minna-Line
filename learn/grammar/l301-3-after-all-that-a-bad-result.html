JLPT N2Unit 1

～あげく

After all that, (a bad result)

How to build it
V た + あげく(に)N の + あげく(に)
What it means

Use V た / N の + あげく(に) when a long, tiring process ends in a disappointing result.

  • After a long, tiring process, an unwelcome result comes.
  • The result is usually negative or disappointing.
  • 結局 or とうとう often sits in the second half.
  • It can't be used for a result you are happy about.
If you sigh at the ending, あげく is the right word.
末に or あげく
PatternResult
～た末にGood or bad
～あげくMostly bad
Example sentences
  1. 長ながい間あいだ迷まよったあげく、結局けっきょく何なにも買かわなかった。Nagai aida mayotta ageku, kekkyoku nani mo kawanakatta.After hesitating for ages, I ended up buying nothing.
  2. 兄あには何度なんども失敗しっぱいしたあげく、仕事しごとをやめてしまった。Ani wa nando mo shippai shita ageku, shigoto o yamete shimatta.After failing again and again, my brother quit his job.
  3. 彼かれはさんざん文句もんくを言いったあげくに、帰かえっていった。Kare wa sanzan monku o itta ageku ni, kaette itta.After complaining endlessly, he went home.
Common mistakes
  • 悩むあげく、会社を辞めた。 → 悩んだあげく、会社を辞めた。
    Use the た-form before あげく.
