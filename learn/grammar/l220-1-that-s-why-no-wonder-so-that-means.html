JLPT N3Unit 20

～わけだ

That's why, no wonder, so that means

How to build it
V・い-adj plain + わけだな-adj な + わけだN の・N という + わけだ
What it means

わけだ draws the natural conclusion from a fact you have just learned or stated.

  • Use it when facts suddenly make sense.
  • どうりで before it means 'no wonder'.
  • It can also state a calculated result.
  • な-adjectives keep な.
Think of わけだ as the sound of a light bulb coming on.
Two uses
JapaneseMeaning
どうりで暗いわけだno wonder it's dark
合計で二十時間になるわけだthat comes to twenty hours
Example sentences
  1. パスワードが違ちがっていたのか。入はいれないわけだ。Pasuwādo ga chigatte ita no ka. Hairenai wake da.The password was wrong? No wonder I couldn't get in.
  2. 毎日まいにち三時間さんじかんネットを見みているなら、一週間いっしゅうかんで二十一時間にじゅういちじかんになるわけだ。Mainichi sanjikan netto o mite iru nara, isshūkan de nijūichijikan ni naru wake da.If you are online three hours a day, that makes twenty-one hours a week.
  3. でんげんが入はいっていない。画面がめんが暗くらいわけだ。Dengen ga haitte inai. Gamen ga kurai wake da.It isn't switched on. So that's why the screen is dark.
Common mistakes
  • 上手だわけだ。 → 上手なわけだ。
    な-adjectives keep な before わけだ.
