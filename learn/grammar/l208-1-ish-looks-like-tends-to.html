JLPT N3Unit 8

～っぽい

-ish, looks like, tends to

How to build it
N + っぽいいA stem + っぽいV ます-stem + っぽい
What it means

Use ～っぽい in casual speech for something with a lot of a quality, or someone with a certain tendency.

  • It conjugates like an いA.
  • With nouns and colours: looks like, has a lot of.
  • With verbs: often does this.
  • It's casual and often a little negative.
子どもっぽい is criticism. 子どもらしい is praise.
Common words
TypeJapaneseMeaning
Colour黒っぽいBlackish
Noun子どもっぽいChildish
Adjective安っぽいCheap-looking
Verb怒りっぽいQuick to anger
Example sentences
  1. 白しろっぽいシャツを探さがしている。Shiroppoi shatsu o sagashite iru.I'm looking for a whitish shirt.
  2. この服ふくは安やすっぽく見みえる。Kono fuku wa yasuppoku mieru.These clothes look cheap.
  3. 最近さいきん、忘わすれっぽくなった。Saikin, wasureppoku natta.I've become forgetful lately.
Common mistakes
  • 白いっぽいシャツ → 白っぽいシャツ
    Drop the い.
  • 忘れるっぽい人 → 忘れっぽい人
    Use the ます-stem.
