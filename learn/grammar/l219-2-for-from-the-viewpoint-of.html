JLPT N3Unit 19

N + にとって

For, from the viewpoint of

How to build it
N + にとって + judgementN + にとっての + N
What it means

にとって gives the person from whose viewpoint something is judged.

  • The noun is usually a person or group.
  • Follow it with words like 大切, 難しい, 便利.
  • It does not take actions or opinions like 賛成.
  • にとっては adds contrast with other people.
If you could replace it with 'from the point of view of', にとって is right.
Natural judgement words
JapaneseMeaning
私にとって大切だimportant to me
学生にとって難しいdifficult for students
親にとって便利だhandy for parents
Example sentences
  1. 社員しゃいんにとって、給料きゅうりょうは大切たいせつな問題もんだいだ。Shain ni totte, kyūryō wa taisetsu na mondai da.For employees, pay is an important issue.
  2. 私わたしにとって、この仕事しごとは夢ゆめだった。Watashi ni totte, kono shigoto wa yume datta.For me, this job was a dream.
  3. この会社かいしゃには、働はたらく親おやにとって便利べんりな制度せいどが多おおい。Kono kaisha ni wa, hataraku oya ni totte benri na seido ga ōi.This company has many systems that are useful for working parents.
Common mistakes
  • 私にとって、賛成です。 → 私は賛成です。
    State your own opinion with は. にとって is for evaluating something.
