JLPT N1Unit 17

～をものともせず

Undaunted by ...

How to build it
N + をものともせずN + をものともしない
What it means

N + をものともせず(に) means to proceed bravely, taking no notice of a hardship.

  • The person defies a hardship and goes on.
  • The tone is admiring.
  • N is something hard: 困難, 反対, 悪天候.
Use it for heroes, athletes and brave people.
Compare
PatternFeel
をものともせずadmiring: not afraid
をよそにcritical: ignoring
Example sentences
  1. 彼かれは周囲しゅういの反対はんたいをものともせず、夢ゆめを追おい続つづけた。Kare wa shūi no hantai o mono to mo sezu, yume o oitsuzuketa.Undaunted by opposition around him, he kept chasing his dream.
  2. 選手せんしゅたちは猛吹雪もうふぶきをものともせず、山頂さんちょうを目指めざした。Senshutachi wa mō-fubuki o mono to mo sezu, sanchō o mezashita.The players aimed for the summit, undaunted by the blizzard.
  3. 彼女かのじょは病やまいをものともせず、舞台ぶたいに立たった。Kanojo wa yamai o mono to mo sezu, butai ni tatta.She stood on the stage in spite of her illness.
Common mistakes
  • 雨をものともせず、傘をさした。 → 雨をものともせず、走った。
    The main clause shows bold action.
