Prohibitive form

Telling someone not to do something

How to build it
V dictionary + な
What it means

The dictionary form + な is a blunt don't, heard in the same situations as the imperative.

  • Just add な to the dictionary form.
  • It works for all three groups.
  • It is as strong as the imperative.
  • Use ～ないで ください in polite speech.
Think of な as a hand held up: stop, don't do that.

Example sentences
  1. ここに 車くるまを 止とめるな。Koko ni kuruma o tomeru na.Don't park your car here!
  2. 駅えきの 中なかで 走はしるな。Eki no naka de hashiru na.Don't run in the station!
  3. 今いま 話はなすな。Ima hanasu na.Don't talk now!
Common mistakes
  • のみな。 (to stop someone) → のむな。
    な joins the dictionary form.
  • こな。 → くるな。
    The dictionary form of きます is くる.
