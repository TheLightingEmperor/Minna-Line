JLPT N2Unit 21

～かねる

Cannot (politely)

How to build it
V ます-stem + かねる / かねます
What it means

Use V ます-stem + かねる to say politely that you are unable or unwilling to do or agree to something.

  • It politely says I'm unable or unwilling to do it.
  • It sounds like business language.
  • It is a softer refusal than できない.
  • かねない is its opposite: may well happen.
Business-polite way to say no without saying 'no'.
Compare
PatternMeaning
賛成しかねるhard to agree
賛成しかねないmay well agree
Example sentences
  1. 申もうし訳わけありませんが、そのご依頼いらいはお引ひき受うけしかねます。Mōshiwake arimasen ga, sono go-irai wa o-hikiuke shikanemasu.I'm sorry, but we cannot take on that request.
  2. 私わたしには、判断はんだんしかねる問題もんだいです。Watashi ni wa, handan shikaneru mondai desu.It is a matter I cannot judge.
  3. その意見いけんには、賛成さんせいしかねる。Sono iken ni wa, sansei shikaneru.I find it hard to agree with that opinion.
Common mistakes
  • 受けかねないです。 → お受けしかねます。
    かねる, not かねない, for polite refusal.
