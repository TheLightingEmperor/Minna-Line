JLPT N1Unit 13

～んばかりの

As if about to ...

How to build it
V ない-stem + んばかりだV ない-stem + んばかりの + N
What it means

V ない-stem + んばかりだ / んばかりの + N means doing something to the point of almost ...ing.

  • The state looks very close to the action.
  • する becomes せんばかり.
  • It describes looks or gestures.
Use it to paint a vivid picture.
Forms
VerbForm
泣く泣かんばかり
するせんばかり
Example sentences
  1. 彼女かのじょは泣なかんばかりの顔かおで謝あやまった。Kanojo wa nakan bakari no kao de ayamatta.She apologised with a face looking about to cry.
  2. 会場かいじょうは割われんばかりの拍手はくしゅに包つつまれた。Kaijō wa waren bakari no hakushu ni tsutsumareta.The hall was wrapped in thunderous applause.
  3. 彼かれは飛とびかからんばかりの勢いきおいで怒おこった。Kare wa tobikakaran bakari no ikioi de okotta.He was so angry he looked ready to leap at someone.
Common mistakes
  • 泣きんばかりの顔 → 泣かんばかりの顔
    Use the ない-stem before んばかり.
