N + まで (even)

Even, as far as

How to build it
N + までN + particle + までV て + まで
What it means

Here まで means 'even': things have gone further than you would expect.

  • It adds one more surprising item.
  • It replaces が and を.
  • It often pairs with だけでなく.
  • Vてまで means going that far is too much.
Think of a list growing longer than you expected. まで adds the surprising last item.
まで in two meanings
JapaneseMeaning
駅まで歩くwalk as far as the station
靴までぬれたeven my shoes got wet
うそをついてまでgoing so far as to lie
Example sentences
  1. 友達ともだちだけでなく、先生せんせいにまで笑わらわれた。Tomodachi dake de naku, sensei ni made warawareta.Not only my friends but even my teacher laughed at me.
  2. 雨あめがざあざあ降ふって、かばんの中なかまでぬれた。Ame ga zāzā futte, kaban no naka made nureta.It poured down, and even the inside of my bag got wet.
  3. 彼かれはうそをついてまでお金かねを借かりた。Kare wa uso o tsuite made okane o karita.He went so far as to lie in order to borrow money.
Common mistakes
  • 靴をまでぬらした。 → 靴までぬらした。
    まで replaces を here.
