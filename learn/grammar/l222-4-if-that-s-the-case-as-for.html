JLPT N3Unit 22

～なら

If that's the case, as for

How to build it
V・い-adj plain + ならな-adj・N + なら
What it means

なら takes up what someone has said or plans, then gives advice or a comment.

  • It often responds to what the other person said.
  • The second half is advice, a request or a view.
  • The action after なら can happen first.
  • な-adjectives and nouns drop だ.
When a friend says 'I'm thinking of…', answer with なら and a suggestion.
なら or たら
JapaneseOrder of events
日本へ行くなら、カメラを買おうbuy first, then go
日本へ行ったら、カメラを買おうgo first, then buy
Example sentences
  1. 京都きょうとへ行いくなら、秋あきがいいですよ。Kyōto e iku nara, aki ga ii desu yo.If you are going to Kyoto, autumn is a good time.
  2. 読よみ終おわったなら、その本ほんを貸かしてください。Yomiowatta nara, sono hon o kashite kudasai.If you have finished reading it, please lend me that book.
  3. すしなら、駅前えきまえの店みせが一番いちばんだ。Sushi nara, ekimae no mise ga ichiban da.If it's sushi you want, the shop by the station is the best.
Common mistakes
  • 日本へ行ったら、ビザを取ってください。 → 日本へ行くなら、ビザを取ってください。
    The visa comes first, so たら does not work.
