JLPT N3Unit 16

～ないと

If you don't ~; I have to ~

How to build it
V ない + と、bad resultV ない + と。
What it means

ないと warns that a bad result follows if something is not done, and alone it means 'I have to'.

  • The second half is a natural, unwelcome result.
  • Do not put a request after it.
  • Alone, it is short for ないといけない.
  • It is spoken and casual.
Say もう行かないと when leaving a friend's house. It is the most natural exit line.
Two uses
JapaneseMeaning
寝ないと、疲れるよif you don't sleep, you'll be tired
もう寝ないとI have to sleep now
Example sentences
  1. 早はやく薬くすりを飲のまないと、熱ねつが下さがらないよ。Hayaku kusuri o nomanai to, netsu ga sagaranai yo.If you don't take your medicine soon, your fever won't go down.
  2. 毎日まいにち運動うんどうしないと、体からだがよわくなる。Mainichi undō shinai to, karada ga yowaku naru.If you don't exercise every day, your body gets weak.
  3. もう寝ねないと。明日あしたは朝あさから病院びょういんだ。Mō nenai to. Ashita wa asa kara byōin da.I've got to go to bed now. I'm at the hospital from the morning tomorrow.
Common mistakes
  • 飲まないと、飲んでください。 → 飲まないと、よくなりませんよ。
    と cannot be followed by a request.
