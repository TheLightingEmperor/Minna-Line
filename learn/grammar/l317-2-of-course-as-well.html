JLPT N2Unit 17

～はもちろん

Of course... as well

How to build it
N1 + はもちろん、N2 + も
What it means

Use N1 はもちろん、N2 も to say N1 is obvious and N2 is true as well.

  • N1 is obvious; N2 is added as a bonus.
  • N2 usually has も.
  • もちろん can also be a standalone adverb.
  • It is close to はもとより, which is more formal.
Of course A — and B too!
Order
PartRole
N1 はもちろんthe obvious one
N2 もthe surprising addition
Example sentences
  1. この町まちは、夏なつはもちろん、冬ふゆも観光客かんこうきゃくが多おおい。Kono machi wa, natsu wa mochiron, fuyu mo kankōkyaku ga ōi.This town has many tourists in winter as well as summer.
  2. 彼かれは日本語にほんごはもちろん、中国語ちゅうごくごも話はなせる。Kare wa Nihongo wa mochiron, Chūgokugo mo hanaseru.He speaks Chinese as well as, of course, Japanese.
  3. SNSは若者わかものはもちろん、お年寄としよりにも使つかわれている。Esu-enu-esu wa wakamono wa mochiron, otoshiyori ni mo tsukawarete iru.Social media is used by the elderly as well as, of course, the young.
Common mistakes
  • 日本語はもちろん、英語を話せる。 → 日本語はもちろん、英語も話せる。
    Use も for the added item.
