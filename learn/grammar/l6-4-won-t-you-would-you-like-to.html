V ませんか

Won't you…? Would you like to…?

How to build it
V ませんかいっしょに V ませんか
What it means

Change ～ます to ～ませんか to invite someone politely — it respects the listener's choice. It often comes with いっしょに (together). Accept with ええ、いいですね; decline softly with すみません、ちょっと…, which hints 'no' without saying it.

  • It invites politely and respects the listener's choice.
  • It often comes with いっしょに, which means together.
  • ちょっと… hints no without saying いいえ.
