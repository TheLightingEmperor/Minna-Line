N1 の N2 (country / maker)

N2 made in N1, N2 made by N1

How to build it
N1 の N2どこの N ですか
What it means

When N1 is a country, 'N1 の N2' says where something comes from: にほんの くるま = a Japanese car. When N1 is a company, it is the maker. Ask with どこの N ('made where / by which company?').

  • Ask with どこの N: made where, or by which company?
  • Answer with N1 の N2.
N1 isExampleEnglish
A countryにほんの くるまa Japanese car
A companyパワーでんきの コンピューターa Power Electric computer
Example sentences
  1. これは にほんの くるまです。Kore wa Nihon no kuruma desu.This is a Japanese car.
  2. これは どこの ワインですか。…イタリアの ワインです。Kore wa doko no wain desu ka. …Itaria no wain desu.Where is this wine from? — It's Italian wine.
  3. それは パワーでんきの コンピューターです。Sore wa Pawā-denki no konpyūtā desu.That's a Power Electric computer.
