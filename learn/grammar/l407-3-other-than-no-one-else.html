JLPT N1Unit 7

～をおいて

Other than ... (no one else)

How to build it
N + をおいて + ない
What it means

N + をおいて (ない) means no one or nothing but N will do.

  • It singles out N as the only choice.
  • The ending is negative: いない, ない, 考えられない.
  • It is formal and emphatic.
Link it with 'the one and only' in your mind.
Compare
PatternMeaning
をおいてnone but N
以外にbesides N
Example sentences
  1. この仕事しごとを任まかせられるのは、彼かれをおいてほかにいない。Kono shigoto o makaserareru no wa, kare o oite hoka ni inai.There is no one other than him to whom I can entrust this job.
  2. この役やくは彼女かのじょをおいて考かんがえられない。Kono yaku wa kanojo o oite kangaerarenai.I can't think of anyone but her for this role.
  3. この町まちをおいて、住すみたい場所ばしょはない。Kono machi o oite, sumitai basho wa nai.There is no place I'd rather live than this town.
Common mistakes
  • 彼をおいて、適任者がいる。 → 彼をおいて、適任者はいない。
    The second half must be negative.
