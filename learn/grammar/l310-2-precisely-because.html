JLPT N2Unit 10

～だけに

Precisely because

How to build it
plain form + だけにN + だけに
What it means

Use plain form + だけに when a situation naturally leads to a fitting result or feeling.

  • The result fits the cause and is as expected.
  • It can be good or bad.
  • It often adds a feeling of admiration or regret.
  • For good outcomes, the close variant だけあって is also used.
A fitting cause: so naturally, the result.
Compare
PatternFeeling
だけにnaturally, as expected
ばかりにunlucky outcome
Example sentences
  1. 彼かれはプロだけに、説明せつめいが上手じょうずだ。Kare wa puro dake ni, setsumei ga jōzu da.He's a pro, so naturally he explains well.
  2. 高たかいだけに、品質ひんしつもいい。Takai dake ni, hinshitsu mo ii.It's expensive, and the quality is good as you'd expect.
  3. 期待きたいしていただけに、失敗しっぱいは残念ざんねんだった。Kitai shite ita dake ni, shippai wa zannen datta.Because I'd expected so much, the failure was disappointing.
Common mistakes
  • プロだけ、説明が上手だ。 → プロだけに、説明が上手だ。
    Keep に at the end of だけに.
