JLPT N2Unit 10

～ばかりに

Just because

How to build it
V / いA plain + ばかりになA な / N の + ばかりに
What it means

Use plain form + ばかりに when one small cause led to an unfortunate result.

  • One small cause brings an unlucky result.
  • It carries regret or complaint.
  • The first half is usually a past fact.
  • It can't lead to good results.
Feel the sigh: 'if only I hadn't...'.
Compare
PatternResult
ばかりにbad, with regret
おかげでgood, with thanks
せいでbad, with blame
Example sentences
  1. 鍵かぎを忘わすれたばかりに、家いえに入はいれなかった。Kagi o wasureta bakari ni, ie ni hairenakatta.Just because I forgot my key, I couldn't get in.
  2. 英語えいごが話はなせないばかりに、その仕事しごとを断ことわられた。Eigo ga hanasenai bakari ni, sono shigoto o kotowarareta.Just because I couldn't speak English, I was turned down.
  3. 言いい過すぎたばかりに、友ともだちを失うしなった。Iisugita bakari ni, tomodachi o ushinatta.I said too much, and lost a friend because of it.
Common mistakes
  • 練習したばかりに、優勝した。 → 練習したおかげで、優勝した。
    ばかりに can't be used for good results.
