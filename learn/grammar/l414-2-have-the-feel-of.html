JLPT N1Unit 14

～めく

Have the feel of ...

How to build it
N + めくN + めいた + N
What it means

N / adj stem + めく means to have the look or feel of N: a hint of it.

  • It shows a faint hint of N.
  • Common: 春めく, 謎めく, 皮肉めく.
  • It is a literary verb.
Learn the set: 春めく, 謎めく, 皮肉めく, 秘密めく.
Common words
WordMeaning
春めくbecome spring-like
謎めくlook mysterious
Example sentences
  1. 春はるめいてきて、日差ひざしが暖あたたかい。Haru meite kite, hizashi ga atatakai.It's getting spring-like and the sun is warm.
  2. 彼かれは謎なぞめいた言葉ことばを残のこした。Kare wa nazo meita kotoba o nokoshita.He left behind some mysterious words.
  3. 皮肉ひにくめいた口調くちょうで彼かれは笑わらった。Hiniku meita kuchō de kare wa waratta.He laughed in a sarcastic tone.
Common mistakes
  • 肉めいた料理 → 謎めいた手紙
    Only a few nouns take めく.
