JLPT N3Unit 21

～こそ

Precisely, this is the one

How to build it
N + こそreason + からこそ
What it means

こそ puts strong emphasis on the word before it: this one and no other.

  • It replaces は, が and を.
  • 今度こそ and 今年こそ show determination.
  • こちらこそ returns thanks or a greeting.
  • からこそ says 'precisely because'.
Say こちらこそ the next time someone thanks you. It is the easiest win in this unit.
Set phrases
JapaneseMeaning
こちらこそthe same to you, no, thank you
今度こそthis time for sure
これこそthis is exactly it
だからこそthat is exactly why
Example sentences
  1. 今年ことしこそ、毎朝まいあさぱっと起おきたい。Kotoshi koso, maiasa patto okitai.This year for sure, I want to spring out of bed every morning.
  2. こちらこそ、どうぞよろしくお願ねがいします。Kochira koso, dōzo yoroshiku onegai shimasu.The pleasure is mine. I look forward to working with you.
  3. 疲つかれているからこそ、ゆっくり休やすむべきだ。Tsukarete iru kara koso, yukkuri yasumu beki da.It is precisely because you are tired that you should rest properly.
Common mistakes
  • 今年をこそがんばる。 → 今年こそがんばる。
    こそ replaces the particle.
