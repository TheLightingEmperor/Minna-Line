JLPT N2Unit 1

～次第

As soon as; depending on

How to build it
V ます-stem + 次第N + 次第だ
What it means

Use V ます-stem + 次第 for 'as soon as X, I will do Y'. Use N + 次第だ for 'it depends on N'.

  • It means as soon as, with no gap in between.
  • The second half is your action or plan, not a past event.
  • N + 次第だ means it all depends on N.
  • It sounds polite, so it is common in business.
Think: the moment X is done, Y starts. Use it for future plans.
Two uses
FormMeaning
分かり次第as soon as I know
お金次第depends on the money
Example sentences
  1. 結果けっかが分わかり次第しだい、ご連絡れんらくします。Kekka ga wakari shidai, go-renraku shimasu.I'll contact you as soon as the results are known.
  2. 駅えきに着つき次第しだい、電話でんわしてください。Eki ni tsuki shidai, denwa shite kudasai.Please call me as soon as you reach the station.
  3. 行いくかどうかは、天気てんき次第しだいです。Iku ka dō ka wa, tenki shidai desu.Whether I go depends on the weather.
Common mistakes
  • 家に着いた次第、電話します。 → 家に着き次第、電話します。
    Use the ます-stem, not the た-form.
