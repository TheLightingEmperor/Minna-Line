JLPT N3Unit 10

～どころか

Far from, let alone

How to build it
V / いA plain + どころかなA / N + どころかN + どころではない
What it means

Use ～どころか to reject an idea and say the reality is the opposite or much more extreme.

  • It denies what the listener expects.
  • The opposite, or something stronger, is true.
  • The second half often uses も or さえ.
  • どころではない means it's no time for that.
Read it as: not only not X, but actually Y.
Two uses
JapaneseMeaning
好きどころか、大きらいだ。Far from liking it, I hate it.
今は旅行どころではない。Now is no time for travel.
Example sentences
  1. 試合しあいで勝かつどころか、一点いってんも取とれなかった。Shiai de katsu dokoro ka, itten mo torenakatta.Far from winning the match, we didn't score a single point.
  2. ピアノどころか、がっきは一ひとつもひけない。Piano dokoro ka, gakki wa hitotsu mo hikenai.Let alone the piano, I can't play a single instrument.
  3. 休やすむどころか、週末しゅうまつも働はたらいている。Yasumu dokoro ka, shūmatsu mo hataraite iru.Far from resting, I'm working at weekends too.
Common mistakes
  • おいしいどころか、おいしい。 → おいしいどころか、まずかった。
    The second half must go the opposite way or further.
