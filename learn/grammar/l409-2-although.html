JLPT N1Unit 9

～ながらも

Although ...

How to build it
V ます-stem + ながらもi-adj + ながらもN + ながらも
What it means

V ます-stem / adj / N + ながらも gives a contrast: the state is true, but the result is unexpected.

  • It joins a fact and an unexpected result.
  • The subject is the same in both halves.
  • It is formal and common in writing.
Use it with small but, poor but, tired but ...
Compare
PatternMeaning
ながらat the same time
ながらもalthough
Example sentences
  1. 狭せまいながらも、楽たのしい我わが家やです。Semai nagara mo, tanoshii wagaya desu.Small though it is, it is my happy home.
  2. 彼かれは体からだが弱よわいながらも、毎日まいにち働はたらいている。Kare wa karada ga yowai nagara mo, mainichi hataraite iru.Although he's weak, he works every day.
  3. 事情じじょうを知しりながらも、彼かれは黙だまっていた。Jijō o shiri nagara mo, kare wa damatte ita.Although he knew the circumstances, he stayed silent.
