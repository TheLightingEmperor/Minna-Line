JLPT N1Unit 11

～すら

Even ...

How to build it
N + すらN + ですら
What it means

N + すら marks an extreme example to show that other things are surely true too.

  • It picks an extreme case.
  • The ending can be negative or positive.
  • It is more literary than さえ.
Swap it with さえ in conversation.
Compare
PatternFeel
すらliterary, extreme
さえeveryday, extreme
Example sentences
  1. 彼かれは自分じぶんの名前なまえすら書かけない。Kare wa jibun no namae sura kakenai.He can't even write his own name.
  2. 忙いそがしくて、寝ねる時間じかんすらない。Isogashikute, neru jikan sura nai.I'm so busy I don't even have time to sleep.
  3. 子供こどもですら知しっている常識じょうしきだ。Kodomo de sura shitte iru jōshiki da.It's common sense even a child knows.
Common mistakes
  • 食事すらする。 → 食事する時間すらない。
    すら usually points to what is not even possible.
