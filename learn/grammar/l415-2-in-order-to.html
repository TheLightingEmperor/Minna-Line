JLPT N1Unit 15

～べく

In order to ...

How to build it
V dictionary form + べくV する → すべく
What it means

V dictionary form + べく expresses a purpose in formal writing: 'with a view to'.

  • It states a purpose and the effort for it.
  • The second half is a deliberate action.
  • The form for する is すべく.
Write-only style; for speech use ために.
Compare
PatternFeel
べくformal purpose
ためにgeneral purpose
Example sentences
  1. 目標もくひょうを達成たっせいすべく、全力ぜんりょくを尽つくした。Mokuhyō o tassei su beku, zenryoku o tsukushita.We did our best in order to achieve the goal.
  2. 彼かれは夢ゆめを叶かなえるべく、留学りゅうがくした。Kare wa yume o kanaeru beku, ryūgaku shita.He studied abroad in order to fulfil his dream.
  3. 問題もんだいを解決かいけつすべく、会議かいぎが開ひらかれた。Mondai o kaiketsu su beku, kaigi ga hirakareta.A meeting was held in order to solve the problem.
Common mistakes
  • 勝つべく、寝たい。 → 勝つべく、練習した。
    The second half is a deliberate action.
