だれ・どなた / なんさい・おいくつ

Who? How old?

How to build it
N は だれですかN は なんさいですか
What it means

だれ means 'who'; どなた is the polite version. なんさい means 'how old'; おいくつ is more polite. Put the question word where the answer will go and keep か at the end — and don't answer question-word questions with はい/いいえ. Watch the readings: 1 = いっさい, 8 = はっさい, 10 = じゅっさい.

  • A question word asks for information, like who or how old.
  • Put it where the answer will go. Keep か at the end.
  • Don't answer these questions with はい or いいえ.
  • さい is a counter: a word added to a number to count.

Example sentences
  1. あのかたは どなたですか。…リンさんです。Ano kata wa donata desu ka. …Rin-san desu.Who is that person? — That's Mr. Lin.
  2. エレナちゃんは なんさいですか。…きゅうさいです。Erena-chan wa nan-sai desu ka. …Kyū-sai desu.How old is Elena? — She's nine.
  3. やまださんは おいくつですか。…よんじゅうごさいです。Yamada-san wa oikutsu desu ka. …Yonjūgo-sai desu.How old are you, Mr. Yamada? — I'm 45.
