JLPT N3Unit 23

Suffering passive

Being troubled by what someone did

How to build it
person + に + V passiveperson + に + N を + V passive
What it means

This passive shows that someone's action or an event caused the speaker trouble.

  • The speaker is the hidden subject.
  • に marks the one who caused the trouble.
  • Even 降る and なく can be passive here.
  • Your body parts and things take を.
Add 'on me' in your head: my child cried on me, the rain fell on me.
Plain or suffering
JapaneseFeeling
雨が降ったplain fact
雨に降られたthe rain caught me
弟がケーキを食べたplain fact
弟にケーキを食べられたmy brother ate my cake
Example sentences
  1. 昨日きのう、雨あめに降ふられて、ぬれてしまった。Kinō, ame ni furarete, nurete shimatta.I got caught in the rain yesterday and got wet.
  2. 電車でんしゃで足あしをふまれた。Densha de ashi o fumareta.Someone stepped on my foot on the train.
  3. 夜中よなかに子供こどもになかれて、眠ねむれなかった。Yonaka ni kodomo ni nakarete, nemurenakatta.My child cried in the night and I couldn't sleep.
Common mistakes
  • 私の足がふまれた。 → 足をふまれた。
    For your own body or things, use を and leave out 私の.
