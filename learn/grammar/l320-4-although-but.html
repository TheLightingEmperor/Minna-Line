JLPT N2Unit 20

～ものの

Although; but

How to build it
plain form + もののN / なA + である + ものの
What it means

Use plain form + ものの when a fact is true but does not lead to the expected result.

  • A fact is true, but the expected result doesn't follow.
  • The second half shows a gap, doubt or disappointment.
  • It sounds written and slightly formal.
  • It's close to けれども but more bookish.
I did X, but... it didn't go as I hoped.
Compare
PatternFeeling
もののadmits X, but Y not as expected
ながらもtwo facts clash
のにcomplaint or surprise
Example sentences
  1. 買かったものの、まだ一度いちども使つかっていない。Katta mono no, mada ichido mo tsukatte inai.I bought it, but I haven't used it even once.
  2. 運動うんどうしなければと思おもうものの、なかなか続つづかない。Undō shinakereba to omou mono no, nakanaka tsuzukanai.I think I should exercise, but I can't keep it up.
  3. 留学りゅうがくは決きめたものの、不安ふあんも多おおい。Ryūgaku wa kimeta mono no, fuan mo ōi.I've decided to study abroad, but I have many worries.
Common mistakes
  • 買ったもののに、使っていない。 → 買ったものの、使っていない。
    Don't add のに after ものの.
