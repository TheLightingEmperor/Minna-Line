JLPT N1Unit 4

～を禁じ得ない

Cannot help feeling ...

How to build it
N + を禁じ得ない
What it means

N + を禁じ得ない expresses a strong feeling that cannot be held back, in a formal register.

  • It means the feeling comes out whether you want it or not.
  • Typical nouns: 涙, 怒り, 驚き, 同情, 笑い.
  • It is a stiff, written expression.
Learn it as a set with 涙, 怒り, 驚き and 同情.
Typical nouns
NounMeaning
涙tears
怒りanger
Example sentences
  1. 被害者ひがいしゃの話はなしを聞きいて、涙なみだを禁きんじ得えなかった。Higaisha no hanashi o kiite, namida o kinjienakatta.Hearing the victim's story, I could not hold back my tears.
  2. 政府せいふの対応たいおうには怒いかりを禁きんじ得えない。Seifu no taiō ni wa ikari o kinjienai.I cannot help feeling anger at the government's response.
  3. 彼かれの死しには驚おどろきを禁きんじ得えない。Kare no shi ni wa odoroki o kinjienai.I cannot suppress my shock at his death.
Common mistakes
  • コーヒーを禁じ得ない。 → 同情を禁じ得ない。
    The noun must be a feeling or a reaction.
