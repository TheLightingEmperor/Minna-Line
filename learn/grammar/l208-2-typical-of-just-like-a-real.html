N + らしい (typical)

Typical of, just like a real

How to build it
N + らしい + NN + らしく + VN + らしくない
What it means

Use noun + らしい to say something has the real, ideal qualities of that noun.

  • It means having the ideal traits of that noun.
  • It's often praise.
  • らしくない shows surprise or disappointment.
  • 自分らしい means true to yourself.
自分らしい服 is a handy phrase for talking about style.

Two kinds of らしい
JapaneseMeaning
彼は本当に男らしい。He is really manly.
あの人は男の人らしい。That person is apparently a man.
Example sentences
  1. 今日きょうは春はるらしい天気てんきだ。Kyō wa harurashii tenki da.Today's weather is very spring-like.
  2. あの人ひとはいつも学生がくせいらしい服ふくを着きている。Ano hito wa itsumo gakuseirashii fuku o kite iru.That person always wears clothes typical of a student.
  3. 約束やくそくを忘わすれるなんて、君きみらしくないね。Yakusoku o wasureru nante, kimi rashiku nai ne.Forgetting a promise isn't like you.
Common mistakes
  • 春のらしい天気 → 春らしい天気
    The noun joins directly.
  • 夏らしいな天気 → 夏らしい天気
    らしい is an いA, so no な.
