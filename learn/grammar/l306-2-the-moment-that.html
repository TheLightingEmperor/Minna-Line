～たとたん(に)

The moment that

How to build it
V た + とたん(に)
What it means

Use V た + とたん(に) when an unexpected event happens at the very moment an action finishes.

  • It means at the very moment after an action ends.
  • The second event is sudden and unplanned.
  • It can't take a request, command or plan.
  • に is optional.
Review: sudden, unplanned, and right after.
What can follow
Second halfFits?

Example sentences
  1. 家いえを出でたとたん、雨あめが降ふり出だした。Ie o deta totan, ame ga furidashita.The moment I left home, it began to rain.
  2. 立たち上あがったとたんに、めまいがした。Tachiagatta totan ni, memai ga shita.The instant I stood up, I felt dizzy.
  3. ドアを開あけたとたん、猫ねこが飛とび出だしてきた。Doa o aketa totan, neko ga tobidashite kita.The moment I opened the door, a cat jumped out.
Common mistakes
  • 窓を開けるとたん、風が入った。 → 窓を開けたとたん、風が入った。
    Always use the た-form before とたん.
