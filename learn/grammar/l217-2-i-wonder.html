JLPT N3Unit 17

～かな

I wonder

How to build it
V・い-adj plain + かなな-adj・N + かなV ない + かな (wish)
What it means

かな shows you are wondering, often to yourself, and ないかな wishes for something.

  • It is casual: use it with friends or alone.
  • な-adjectives and nouns drop だ.
  • ないかな wishes for something to happen.
  • Use でしょうか in polite speech.
Use 行こうかな to think aloud about a plan: volitional + かな means 'maybe I'll ~'.
Wondering or wishing
JapaneseMeaning
晴れるかなI wonder if it'll be sunny
晴れないかなI wish it would clear up
元気かなI wonder if they are well
Example sentences
  1. 彼女かのじょ、怒おこっているかな。Kanojo, okotte iru ka na.I wonder if she is angry.
  2. 明日あしたは晴はれるかな。Ashita wa hareru ka na.I wonder if it will be sunny tomorrow.
  3. 早はやく夏休なつやすみにならないかな。Hayaku natsuyasumi ni naranai ka na.I wish the summer holidays would hurry up and come.
Common mistakes
  • 元気だかな。 → 元気かな。
    Drop だ before かな.
