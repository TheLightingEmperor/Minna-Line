～って (I heard)

They say, I heard (casual)

How to build it
Plain + ってPlain + って + 言う / 聞くN + って + N
What it means

Use ～って in casual speech to pass on what you heard, or in place of と and という.

  • It's the casual form of と, という and そうだ.
  • At the end of a sentence, it means hearsay.
  • って言ってた means someone said so.
  • Keep it out of formal speech and writing.
A rising って？ asks for confirmation: you mean ...?
Casual and standard
CasualStandardMeaning
雨だって。雨だそうです。They say it will rain.
行くって言ってた。行くと言っていました。He said he'd go.
モチって猫モチという猫A cat called Mochi
Example sentences
  1. あの店みせ、来週らいしゅう閉しまるって。Ano mise, raishū shimaru tte.I heard that shop is closing next week.
  2. たなかさん、今日きょうは休やすむって。Tanaka-san, kyō wa yasumu tte.Tanaka says she's taking today off.
  3. 電話でんわで明日あしたとどくって言いってたよ。Denwa de ashita todoku tte itteta yo.They said on the phone that it'll arrive tomorrow.
Common mistakes
  • 部長、明日は雨だって。 → 部長、明日は雨だそうです。
    Too casual for your boss.
