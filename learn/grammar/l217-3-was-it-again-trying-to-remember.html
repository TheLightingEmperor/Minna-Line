JLPT N3Unit 17

～っけ

Was it ~ again? (trying to remember)

How to build it
plain past + っけN・な-adj + だっけ・だったっけpolite past + っけ
What it means

っけ asks about something you once knew but can't quite remember, or recalls the past fondly.

  • Use it for things you have forgotten.
  • Use it with friends. It is casual.
  • だっけ and だったっけ both work.
  • Polite form: でしたっけ, ましたっけ.
When a word escapes you, say 何だっけ out loud. It buys time and sounds natural.
Forms
JapaneseMeaning
何時だっけwhat time was it?
言ったっけdid I say that?
どこでしたっけwhere was it? (polite)
行きましたっけdid we go? (polite)
Example sentences
  1. 今日きょうは何曜日なんようびだっけ。Kyō wa nan'yōbi dakke.What day is it today again?
  2. あの人ひとの名前なまえ、何なんだっけ。Ano hito no namae, nan dakke.What was that person's name again?
  3. 子供こどものころ、よくここで遊あそんだっけ。Kodomo no koro, yoku koko de asondakke.We used to play here a lot as children, didn't we?
Common mistakes
  • 初めて会う人に、お名前は何だっけ。 → お名前は何ですか。
    っけ means you once knew it, so it cannot ask something new.
