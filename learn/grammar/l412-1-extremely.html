JLPT N1Unit 12

～極まりない

Extremely ...

How to build it
na-adj stem + 極まりないna-adj stem + 極まる
What it means

na-adj stem / i-adj の + 極まりない shows an extreme degree, usually negative. Also 極まる (stem + 極まる).

  • The degree is as high as it can get.
  • It mostly follows words with a negative sense.
  • The form 極まる joins in the same way.
Set 失礼極まりない, 危険極まりない, 迷惑極まりない.
Typical words
WordMeaning
失礼rude
危険dangerous
Example sentences
  1. 彼かれの態度たいどは失礼しつれい極きわまりない。Kare no taido wa shitsurei kiwamarinai.His attitude is extremely rude.
  2. 事故じこの現場げんばは危険きけん極きわまりなかった。Jiko no genba wa kiken kiwamarinakatta.The accident scene was extremely dangerous.
  3. 手続てつづきが複雑ふくざつ極きわまりない。Tetsuzuki ga fukuzatsu kiwamarinai.The procedure is extremely complicated.
Common mistakes
  • 親切極まりない人だ。 → 失礼極まりない人だ。
    極まりない usually goes with negative words.
