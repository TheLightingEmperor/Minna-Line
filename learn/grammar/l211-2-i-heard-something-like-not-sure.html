～とか (I heard something like)

I heard something like (not sure)

How to build it
Plain + とかPlain + とか + 聞く / 言う
What it means

Use ～とか at the end of a sentence to pass on something you heard but don't remember exactly.

  • It's vaguer and softer than そうだ.
  • You aren't sure of the details.
  • It's common in small talk.
  • Nouns and なA take だ: 休みだとか.
Use it when you only half remember the news.
Two kinds of とか
UseJapaneseMeaning
Hearsayけっこんするとか。I heard they're getting married.
List犬とか猫とかが好きだ。I like dogs, cats and so on.
Example sentences
  1. こづつみは明日あした着つくとか。Kozutsumi wa ashita tsuku to ka.I heard something about the parcel arriving tomorrow.
  2. 銀行ぎんこうが来月らいげつ引ひっ越こすとか聞ききました。Ginkō ga raigetsu hikkosu to ka kikimashita.I heard the bank is moving next month or something.
  3. 田中たなかさんが入院にゅういんしたとか言いっていた。Tanaka-san ga nyūin shita to ka itte ita.Someone was saying Tanaka has gone into hospital or something.
Common mistakes
  • 明日は雨だとかです。 → 明日は雨だとか。
    End the sentence with とか.
