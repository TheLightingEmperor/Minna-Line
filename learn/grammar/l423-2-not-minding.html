JLPT N1Unit 23

～をいとわず

Not minding ...

How to build it
N + をいとわずV dictionary form + のをいとわず
What it means

N + をいとわず(に) means doing something without caring about the hardship it brings.

  • The person accepts hardship for a goal.
  • The tone is admiring.
  • N is a cost: 手間, 苦労, 危険.
Use it in praise of dedicated people.
Compare
PatternFeel
をいとわずadmires effort
をものともせずdefies a difficulty
Example sentences
  1. 彼かれは苦労くろうをいとわず、働はたらき続つづけた。Kare wa kurō o itowazu, hatarakitsuzuketa.He kept working without minding the hardship.
  2. ボランティアたちは労ろうをいとわず、被災地ひさいちを支ささえた。Borantia-tachi wa rō o itowazu, hisaichi o sasaeta.The volunteers spared no effort in supporting the disaster area.
  3. 彼女かのじょは時間じかんをいとわず、何度なんども説明せつめいしてくれた。Kanojo wa jikan o itowazu, nando mo setsumei shite kureta.She kindly explained again and again, not minding the time.
