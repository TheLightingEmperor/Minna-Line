JLPT N2Unit 14

～て初めて

Only after

How to build it
V て + 初めて
What it means

Use V て + 初めて when you only realise or experience something after an action.

  • It means only after doing, then the realisation comes.
  • The second half is a realisation, a feeling or a new state.
  • It can't be followed by a request or a plan.
  • It often goes with 分かる, 気づく, 知る.
First the experience, then the awakening.
Typical endings
EndingMeaning
分かったrealised
気づいたnoticed
知ったlearned
Example sentences
  1. 病気びょうきになって初はじめて、健康けんこうの大切たいせつさが分わかった。Byōki ni natte hajimete, kenkō no taisetsusa ga wakatta.Only after getting sick did I understand the value of health.
  2. 外国がいこくに住すんで初はじめて、日本にほんのよさに気きづいた。Gaikoku ni sunde hajimete, Nihon no yosa ni kizuita.Only after living abroad did I notice Japan's good points.
  3. 先生せんせいになって初はじめて、教おしえる難むずかしさを知しった。Sensei ni natte hajimete, oshieru muzukashisa o shitta.Only after becoming a teacher did I learn how hard teaching is.
Common mistakes
  • 失った初めて大切さが分かる。 → 失って初めて大切さが分かる。
    Use the て-form before 初めて.
