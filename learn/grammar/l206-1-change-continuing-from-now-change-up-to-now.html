～ていく / ～てくる (change)

Change continuing from now / change up to now

How to build it
V て + くる (up to now)V て + いく (from now on)
What it means

Use ～てくる for a change that has come up to now, and ～ていく for a change that will go on from now.

  • くる brings the change toward now.
  • いく sends the change into the future.
  • It works with change verbs: 増える, 変わる, なる.
  • くる also shows something has just begun.
Imagine a timeline: くる arrives at you, いく moves away.
Three uses
TimeJapaneseMeaning
Up to now寒くなってきた。It has got colder.
From now on寒くなっていく。It will keep getting colder.
Just started雨が降ってきた。It has started raining.
Example sentences
  1. この町まちは最近さいきん、人ひとが増ふえてきた。Kono machi wa saikin, hito ga fuete kita.The number of people in this town has been growing lately.
  2. これからも古ふるい店みせはへっていくだろう。Kore kara mo furui mise wa hette iku darō.Old shops will probably keep decreasing from now on.
  3. 駅前えきまえがだんだんにぎやかになってきた。Ekimae ga dandan nigiyaka ni natte kita.The area in front of the station has gradually got livelier.
Common mistakes
  • 町が変わりてきた。 → 町が変わってきた。
    Use the て-form.
  • だんだん暗いなってきた。 → だんだん暗くなってきた。
    いA change to く before なる.
