JLPT N1Unit 22

～に即して

In line with ...; based on

How to build it
N + に即してN + に即した + N
What it means

N + に即して means doing something following actual conditions, rules or facts.

  • The action follows an actual standard.
  • N is a fact, a rule or a situation.
  • It is common in law and policy.
Think of 'tracking the real conditions'.
Compare
PatternFeel
に即してfollow the facts or rules
に沿ってfollow along a guideline
Example sentences
  1. 現実げんじつに即そくして、計画けいかくを立たて直なおす。Genjitsu ni sokushite, keikaku o tatenaosu.We will redo the plan in line with reality.
  2. 法律ほうりつに即そくして判断はんだんする。Hōritsu ni sokushite handan suru.We judge in accordance with the law.
  3. 実情じつじょうに即そくした対策たいさくが必要ひつようだ。Jitsujō ni sokushita taisaku ga hitsuyō da.Measures suited to the actual situation are needed.
