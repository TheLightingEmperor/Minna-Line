～なり

As soon as ... (unexpected action)

How to build it
V dictionary form + なり
What it means

V dictionary form + なり describes a sudden, surprising action right after the first one.

  • Both actions have the same subject.
  • The second action is sudden and unexpected.
  • It cannot be used for the speaker's own will or wishes.
A surprise action by one person right after another action.
Compare
PatternSubject
なりsame subject only
が早いかcan differ
Example sentences
  1. 彼かれは私わたしの顔かおを見みるなり、怒おこり出だした。Kare wa watashi no kao o miru nari, okoridashita.As soon as he saw my face, he began to get angry.
  2. 妹いもうとは家いえに帰かえるなり、部屋へやに閉とじこもった。Imōto wa ie ni kaeru nari, heya ni tojikomotta.As soon as my sister got home, she shut herself in her room.
  3. 彼かれは席せきに座すわるなり、大おおきなあくびをした。Kare wa seki ni suwaru nari, ōkina akubi o shita.As soon as he sat down, he gave a big yawn.
Common mistakes
  • 私が帰るなり、母が怒った。 → 彼は帰るなり、怒り出した。
    なり needs the same subject in both clauses.
