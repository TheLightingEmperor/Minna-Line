JLPT N1Unit 6

～とあって

Because it is a special case

How to build it
N + とあってV / adj plain + とあって
What it means

N / clause + とあって gives the special situation as the reason for a notable result.

  • The reason is an unusual or special situation.
  • The result describes a noticeable state.
  • The second half is not a wish or command.
Think of holidays, events and famous people as the typical triggers.

Example sentences
  1. 連休れんきゅうとあって、駅えきは大変たいへんな混雑こんざつだ。Renkyū to atte, eki wa taihen na konzatsu da.Because it's a long holiday, the station is terribly crowded.
  2. 人気にんき歌手かしゅの来日らいにちとあって、空港くうこうには大勢おおぜいのファンが集あつまった。Ninki kashu no rainichi to atte, kūkō ni wa ōzei no fan ga atsumatta.Because the popular singer was visiting Japan, many fans gathered at the airport.
  3. 初はじめての海外かいがい旅行りょこうとあって、彼かれはわくわくしていた。Hajimete no kaigai ryokō to atte, kare wa wakuwaku shite ita.Because it was his first trip abroad, he was thrilled.
Common mistakes
  • 休みとあって、行きましょう。 → 休みとあって、道が混んでいる。
    Use a description, not an invitation, as the result.
