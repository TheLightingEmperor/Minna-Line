JLPT N1Unit 17

～と相まって

Combined with ...

How to build it
N + と相まって
What it means

N + と相まって says that two factors work together and produce a result.

  • Two factors work together to make the result bigger.
  • The result is often an effect or change.
  • It is common in news and analysis.
Use it when A and B together explain C.
Compare

Example sentences
  1. 景気けいきの回復かいふくと相あいまって、消費しょうひが伸のびている。Keiki no kaifuku to aimatte, shōhi ga nobite iru.Combined with the economic recovery, spending is growing.
  2. 彼かれの才能さいのうは努力どりょくと相あいまって、大おおきな成果せいかを生うんだ。Kare no sainō wa doryoku to aimatte, ōkina seika o unda.His talent, combined with effort, produced great results.
  3. 美うつくしい景色けしきは、静しずけさと相あいまって心こころを癒いやす。Utsukushii keshiki wa, shizukesa to aimatte kokoro o iyasu.The beautiful view, together with the quiet, heals the mind.
