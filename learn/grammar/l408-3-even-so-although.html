JLPT N1Unit 8

～とはいえ

Even so; although ...

How to build it
N + とはいえplain + とはいえ
What it means

Clause + とはいえ admits a fact, then adds something that goes against it.

  • It accepts a fact, then says something unexpected.
  • It is written and formal.
  • The second half is the speaker's real point.
Read it as 'that said' in English.
Similar
PatternFeel
とはいえwritten, formal
といってもsofter, spoken
Example sentences
  1. 春はるとはいえ、まだ朝晩あさばんは冷ひえる。Haru to wa ie, mada asaban wa hieru.Although it is spring, mornings and evenings are still chilly.
  2. 初心者しょしんしゃとはいえ、基本きほんは守まもるべきだ。Shoshinsha to wa ie, kihon wa mamoru beki da.Even if you are a beginner, you should follow the basics.
  3. 休日きゅうじつとはいえ、仕事しごとのメールは見みる。Kyūjitsu to wa ie, shigoto no mēru wa miru.Even though it's a holiday, I check work emails.
Common mistakes
  • 春とはいえ、暖かい。 → 春とはいえ、まだ寒い。
    The second half must go against the first.
