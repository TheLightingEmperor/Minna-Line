JLPT N3Unit 21

N + さえ

Even

How to build it
N + さえN + でさえN + particle + さえ
What it means

さえ picks an extreme example, implying that everything else is true as well.

  • さえ replaces が and を.
  • It follows に and で: 友達にさえ.
  • For a subject, でさえ is common.
  • Do not confuse it with さえ～ば.
Picture the lowest bar on the list. If even that fails, さえ is your word.
With particles
JapaneseMeaning
水さえ飲めないcan't even drink water
子供でさえわかるeven a child understands
親にさえ言わないdoesn't tell even their parents
Example sentences
  1. 彼かれは忙いそがしくて、ゆっくり寝ねる時間じかんさえない。Kare wa isogashikute, yukkuri neru jikan sae nai.He is so busy that he doesn't even have time to get a good sleep.
  2. この問題もんだいは子供こどもでさえわかる。Kono mondai wa kodomo de sae wakaru.Even a child can understand this problem.
  3. どきどきして、水みずさえ飲のめなかった。Dokidoki shite, mizu sae nomenakatta.My heart was pounding so much that I couldn't even drink water.
Common mistakes
  • 親さえに言わない。 → 親にさえ言わない。
    さえ comes after に.
