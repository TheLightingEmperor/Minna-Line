～そばから

No sooner than ... (then undone again)

How to build it
V dictionary form + そばからV た + そばから
What it means

V dictionary form / た + そばから means that each time one thing is done, it is undone or forgotten again.

  • The action repeats and keeps being undone.
  • The tone is often frustrated or helpless.
  • It describes habitual repeated cases, not one event.
The picture is a loop: do it, undo it, do it again.
Compare
PatternFeel
そばからdone then undone, repeated
が早いかone quick event
Example sentences
  1. 覚おぼえるそばから忘わすれてしまう。Oboeru soba kara wasurete shimau.No sooner do I memorise something than I forget it.
  2. 片付かたづけるそばから、子供こどもが散ちらかす。Katazukeru soba kara, kodomo ga chirakasu.The moment I tidy up, the children make a mess again.
  3. 雪ゆきは降ふるそばから溶とけていく。Yuki wa furu soba kara tokete iku.The snow melts as soon as it falls.
Common mistakes
  • 昨日掃除したそばから、汚れた。 → 掃除するそばから、汚れてしまう。
    そばから describes a repeated cycle, not one past event.
