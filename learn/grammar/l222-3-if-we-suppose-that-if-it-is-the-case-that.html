JLPT N3Unit 22

～としたら・～とすれば

If we suppose that, if it is the case that

How to build it
plain + としたらplain + とすればN・な-adj + だとしたら
What it means

としたら and とすれば set up an assumption and then say what would follow.

  • The two mean almost the same.
  • もし often comes at the start.
  • Nouns and な-adjectives keep だ.
  • Often used for unreal or uncertain ideas.
Start a fun question with もし～としたら. It is a perfect conversation opener.
Two uses
JapaneseMeaning
外国に住むとしたらimagining: if I were to live abroad
彼が来ないとすればreasoning: if he really isn't coming
本当だとすればreasoning: if it is true
Example sentences
  1. もしいちおくえん手てに入はいるとしたら、何なにに使つかいますか。Moshi ichiokuen te ni hairu to shitara, nani ni tsukaimasu ka.If you were to get a hundred million yen, what would you spend it on?
  2. 電車でんしゃが止とまっているとすれば、歩あるいて帰かえるしかない。Densha ga tomatte iru to sureba, aruite kaeru shika nai.If the trains have stopped, I will have no choice but to walk home.
  3. 彼かれの話はなしが本当ほんとうだとすれば、計画けいかくを立たて直なおす必要ひつようがある。Kare no hanashi ga hontō da to sureba, keikaku o tatenaosu hitsuyō ga aru.If what he says is true, we need to rework the plan.
Common mistakes
  • 本物なとしたら → 本物だとしたら
    Use だ, not な, before としたら.
