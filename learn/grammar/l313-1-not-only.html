JLPT N2Unit 13

～に限らず

Not only

How to build it
N + に限らず(も)
What it means

Use N + に限らず when you say the point is true for more than just N.

  • It says the point isn't limited to one thing.
  • The second half names another case, often with も.
  • It is neutral and works in speech and writing.
  • It is close to のみならず but less formal.
The circle is bigger than one item.
Compare
PatternRegister
に限らずneutral

ばかりでなくeveryday
Example sentences
  1. この店みせは、休日きゅうじつに限かぎらず、平日へいじつも込こんでいる。Kono mise wa, kyūjitsu ni kagirazu, heijitsu mo konde iru.This shop is crowded on weekdays as well as holidays.
  2. 若わかい人ひとに限かぎらず、お年寄としよりもスマホを使つかっている。Wakai hito ni kagirazu, otoshiyori mo sumaho o tsukatte iru.Not only young people but the elderly also use smartphones.
  3. 彼かれは、勉強べんきょうに限かぎらず、スポーツも得意とくいだ。Kare wa, benkyō ni kagirazu, supōtsu mo tokui da.He's good not only at studying but at sports too.
Common mistakes
  • 子どもを限らず大人も楽しめる。 → 子どもに限らず大人も楽しめる。
    Use に before 限らず.
