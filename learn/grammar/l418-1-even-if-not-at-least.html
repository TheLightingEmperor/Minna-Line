JLPT N1Unit 18

～ないまでも

Even if not ... at least

How to build it
V ない + までもadj ない + までも
What it means

V ない-form + までも, A shows that even if the full degree is not reached, a lesser level is expected.

  • The full level may not be reached.
  • A smaller level is still expected or hoped.
  • The second half is a request, hope or fact.
Think: 'not X, but at least Y'.
Compare
PatternFeel
ないまでもnot X, at least Y
ないにしてもeven if not
Example sentences
  1. 毎日まいにちとは言いわないまでも、週しゅうに三回さんかいは運動うんどうしてほしい。Mainichi to wa iwanai made mo, shū ni sankai wa undō shite hoshii.Not every day perhaps, but I'd like you to exercise three times a week.
  2. 優勝ゆうしょうはできないまでも、入賞にゅうしょうしたい。Yūshō wa dekinai made mo, nyūshō shitai.Even if I can't win, I'd like to place.
  3. 完璧かんぺきとは言いえないまでも、合格点ごうかくてんは取とれた。Kanpeki to wa ienai made mo, gōkakuten wa toreta.It's not perfect, but I did reach a passing mark.
Common mistakes
  • 優勝できないまでも、優勝したい。 → 優勝できないまでも、入賞したい。
    The second half should be a smaller goal.
