JLPT N2Unit 13

～ばかりでなく

Not only... but also

How to build it
V / adj plain + ばかりでなくN + ばかりでなく
What it means

Use plain form + ばかりでなく for 'not just X but also Y', in speech and writing.

  • It adds one more point to a first point.
  • The second part often has も or まで.
  • Plain forms come before it; nouns link directly.
  • ばかりでなく is a little stiffer than だけでなく.
Add another plus on top of the first.
Related
PatternRegister

Example sentences
  1. 彼女かのじょは英語えいごばかりでなく、中国語ちゅうごくごも話はなせる。Kanojo wa eigo bakari de naku, Chūgokugo mo hanaseru.She speaks not only English but also Chinese.
  2. このアパートは、安やすいばかりでなく、駅えきにも近ちかい。Kono apāto wa, yasui bakari de naku, eki ni mo chikai.This apartment is not only cheap but also close to the station.
  3. 彼かれは頭あたまがいいばかりでなく、性格せいかくもいい。Kare wa atama ga ii bakari de naku, seikaku mo ii.He is not only smart but also has a good personality.
Common mistakes
  • 安いばかりでなく、高い。 → 安いばかりでなく、おいしい。
    Both halves should point the same direction.
