JLPT N1Unit 7

～ゆえに

Because of ...; therefore

How to build it
N + ゆえにV / adj plain + がゆえにN + ゆえの + N
What it means

N / plain form + ゆえに is a literary way to give a reason. Often a hard fact or logical result.

  • It sounds like writing, not speech.
  • It gives a hard reason, often a cause of a problem.
  • がゆえに links a plain form to the reason.
Use it in essays; ので and から are for talk.
Forms
FormExample
N ゆえに貧しさゆえに
V がゆえに人間であるがゆえに
Example sentences
  1. 彼かれは貧まずしさゆえに、学校がっこうを辞やめた。Kare wa mazushisa yue ni, gakkō o yameta.Because of poverty, he left school.
  2. 人間にんげんであるがゆえに、悩なやみは尽つきない。Ningen de aru ga yue ni, nayami wa tsukinai.Precisely because we are human, our worries never end.
  3. 若わかさゆえの過あやまちを犯おかした。Wakasa yue no ayamachi o okashita.I made a mistake born of youth.
Common mistakes
  • 雨ゆえに、遊びに行きなさい。 → 雨ゆえに、試合は中止となった。
    The result is a fact, not a wish.
