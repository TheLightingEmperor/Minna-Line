JLPT N1Unit 12

～しまつだ

It ended up that ...

How to build it
V dictionary form + しまつだこの / あの + しまつだ
What it means

V + しまつだ is a bad outcome after things got worse and worse.

  • It says things reached a bad end.
  • The tone is critical or resigned.
  • Past form しまつだった is common.
Picture a snowball: small troubles roll into a big one.
Compare
PatternFeel
しまつだbad end after a build-up
ことになるsimply an outcome
Example sentences
  1. 彼かれは借金しゃっきんを重かさね、とうとう家いえまで売うるしまつだ。Kare wa shakkin o kasane, tōtō ie made uru shimatsu da.He piled up debt and ended up even selling his house.
  2. 注意ちゅういしても聞きかず、最後さいごには怒おこり出だすしまつだ。Chūi shite mo kikazu, saigo ni wa okoridasu shimatsu da.He doesn't listen to warnings, and in the end gets angry.
  3. 遅刻ちこくが続つづき、ついに首くびになるしまつだった。Chikoku ga tsuzuki, tsuini kubi ni naru shimatsu datta.Being late kept up, and in the end he got fired.
Common mistakes
  • 努力して、優勝するしまつだ。 → 借金を重ね、家を売るしまつだ。
    しまつだ is for bad outcomes only.
