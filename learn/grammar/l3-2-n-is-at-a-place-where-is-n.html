N は Place です / どこ

N is at a place. Where is N?

How to build it
N は Place ですN は どこですか
What it means

'N は ここ/そこ/あそこ です' tells where a thing, place or person is. Ask with 'N は どこですか'. Floors use ～かい, but 3rd floor is さんがい and 'which floor' is なんがい.

  • This works for things, places and people.
  • Answer with ここ, そこ, あそこ or a place name.
  • Floors use the counter かい. Watch the sound changes.

Example sentences
  1. トイレは あそこです。Toire wa asoko desu.The toilet is over there.
  2. しょくどうは どこですか。…ちかです。Shokudō wa doko desu ka. …Chika desu.Where is the cafeteria? — It's in the basement.
  3. やまださんは じむしょです。Yamada-san wa jimusho desu.Mr. Yamada is in the office.
