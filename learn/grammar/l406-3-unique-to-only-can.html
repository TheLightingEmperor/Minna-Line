JLPT N1Unit 6

～ならではの

Unique to ...; only ... can

How to build it
N + ならではの + NN + ならでは
What it means

N + ならではの + N (or ならでは) praises something as possible only because of N.

  • It praises something that only N can provide.
  • N is usually a person, place or shop.
  • The tone is positive.
It is common in travel and food reviews.
Compare
PatternFeel

Example sentences
  1. この味あじは、老舗しにせならではのものだ。Kono aji wa, shinise narade wa no mono da.This taste is something only a long-established shop can offer.
  2. 京都きょうとならではの景色けしきを楽たのしんだ。Kyōto narade wa no keshiki o tanoshinda.We enjoyed scenery unique to Kyoto.
  3. 職人しょくにんならではの技わざに感心かんしんした。Shokunin narade wa no waza ni kanshin shita.I was impressed by skills only a craftsman has.
