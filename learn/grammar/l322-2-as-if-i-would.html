JLPT N2Unit 22

～ものか

As if I would

How to build it
plain form + ものかplain form + ものですか (polite)
What it means

Use plain form + ものか for a strong, emotional denial or refusal; it means 'no way I would'.

  • It means I would never, or no way that.
  • It's emotional and used in speech.
  • It's like a rhetorical question: who would...?
  • もんか is its casual form.
A rhetorical 'as if!'
Forms
FormExample
ものか負けるものか
もんか負けるもんか
ものですか負けるものですか
Example sentences
  1. あんな人ひとに負まけるものか。Anna hito ni makeru mono ka.There's no way I'll lose to someone like that.
  2. 誰だれがそんな話はなしを信しんじるものか。Dare ga sonna hanashi o shinjiru mono ka.Who on earth would believe a story like that?
  3. 二度にどとあんな店みせに行いくものか。Nido to anna mise ni iku mono ka.I'll never go to a shop like that again.
Common mistakes
  • 負けないものか。 → 負けるものか。
    Use the plain affirmative form, not the negative.
