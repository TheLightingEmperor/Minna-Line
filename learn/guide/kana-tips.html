かな · Kana Tips

Small details of spelling and sound change the meaning of a word. This page covers the ones that trip people up.

Long vowels in hiragana

A long vowel lasts twice as long as a short one and takes up a whole extra letter.

SoundAdded letterExampleMeaning
Long aあおかあさんmother
Long iいおにいさんolder brother
Long uうくうこうairport
Long e (a few words)えおねえさんolder sister
Long e (most words)いせんせいteacher
Long o (most words)うおとうさんfather
Long o (a few words)おおおきいbig
Most long o sounds are spelled with う. Memorise the N5 ones spelled with お: おおきい, おおい, とおい, とお, おおぜい.

Small っ: a tiny pause

Small っ is a silent beat. Stop for one beat, then hit the next consonant hard. In romaji it doubles that consonant.

WithoutMeaningWithMeaning
おとsoundおっとhusband
きてcomeきってstamp
いてbeいってgo
みつhoneyみっつthree things
When typing, double the next letter: kitte, zasshi, gakkou. Before ch, type tch or cch: matcha.

Small ゃ ゅ ょ: one beat, not two

A small ゃ, ゅ or ょ joins the letter before it into one sound. A full-size one is its own beat.

Full sizeMeaningSmallMeaning
びよういんbeauty salonびょういんhospital
じゆうfreedomじゅうten
りようuseりょうdormitory
びょういん has four beats: byo, u, i, n. Write the small one clearly smaller, or you've gone to the wrong building.

ん changes with the next sound

ん is one full beat, and its sound shifts to match what comes next. You don't need to force this, but it explains what you hear.

Next soundSounds likeExampleMeaning
m, b, pmしんぶんnewspaper
m, b, pmえんぴつpencil
t, d, n, r, s, znほんとうtruth
t, d, n, r, s, znおんなwoman
k, gngぎんこうbank
k, gngにほんごJapanese language
A vowel, y or wa nasal humでんわtelephone
End of a worda soft nasal nほんbook
ん before a vowel or y stays separate: きんえん is ki-n-e-n and ほんや is ho-n-ya. When typing, use n' there: kin'en, hon'ya.

は, へ and を as particles

Three letters read differently when they are particles.

LetterAs a particleInside a wordExampleMeaning
はwahaははは はなが すきです。My mother likes flowers.
へeheへやへ いきます。I go to the room.
をoalmost never usedパンを たべます。I eat bread.
こんにちは and こんばんは keep the particle spelling, so they end in the sound wa. In the first example the word はは is 'mother' and the third は is the particle.

Romaji for し, ち, つ and friends

Some kana don't follow the row's letter in the romaji this game shows. The game also accepts the second spelling when you type.

KanaRomajiAlso typed asExampleMeaning
しshisiしおsalt
ちchitiちずmap
つtsutuつくえdesk
ふfuhuふゆwinter
じjiziじしょdictionary
しゃshasyaいしゃdoctor
ちゃchatyaおちゃtea
じゃjazyaじゃwell then
つ is one beat with the t and s said together. Keep it apart from す: つき is the moon.

ー in katakana

Katakana marks a long vowel with a bar, not an extra vowel letter.

The long stroke of シ and ン sweeps upward, like the end of hiragana し. The long stroke of ツ and ソ sweeps downward, like hiragana つ.
