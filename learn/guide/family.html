かぞく · Family Words

Japanese has one set of words for your own family and a politer set for someone else's. Use the plain set when talking about yours to other people.

Listen for the long vowel: おじいさん is a grandfather and おじさん is an uncle. おばあさん is a grandmother and おばさん is an aunt.

Using them in a sentence

Whose familyExampleMeaning
Mineちちは ぎんこういんです。My father is a bank employee.
Yoursおとうさんは おげんきですか。How is your father?
Mineあねは とうきょうに すんで います。My older sister lives in Tokyo.
Yoursおねえさんは どこに すんで いますか。Where does your older sister live?
Mineつまは せんせいです。My wife is a teacher.
Yoursおくさんの おしごとは なんですか。What does your wife do for work?

Inside the family

When you speak to your own older relatives, use the polite column. Younger relatives are simply called by name.

SituationExampleMeaning
Speaking to your own fatherおとうさん、いって きます。Dad, I'm off.
Speaking to your own motherおかあさん、ただいま。Mum, I'm home.
Speaking to your own older brotherおにいさん、これ、おしえて。Show me how to do this, big brother.
Telling a guest about your motherははは いま でかけて います。My mother is out at the moment.
