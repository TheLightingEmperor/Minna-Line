オノマトペ · Sound Words

Japanese has hundreds of sound-symbolic words. Some copy a real sound; many describe a feeling, a texture or a way of moving. You'll hear them in every conversation, comic and advert.

How they work

Most are a two-beat sound said twice. They slot into a sentence in three common ways.

PatternExampleMeaning
Followed by desuおなかが ぺこぺこです。I'm starving.
Followed by shimasuむねが どきどきします。My heart is pounding.
In front of a verbあめが ざあざあ ふって います。The rain is pouring down.
