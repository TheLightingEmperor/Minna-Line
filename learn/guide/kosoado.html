こそあど · Ko-so-a-do

This set of words points at things. Once you know the four starting sounds, every row of the chart works the same way.

The chart

こ is near the speaker, そ is near the listener, あ is away from both, and ど asks which.

What it points atこ: near meそ: near youあ: away from usど: which?
A thing (stands alone)これそれあれどれ
A thing (needs a noun after it)このそのあのどの
A placeここそこあそこどこ
A direction, or a polite place or personこちらそちらあちらどちら
A direction or choice (casual)こっちそっちあっちどっち
A kind of thing (needs a noun after it)こんなそんなあんなどんな
One row breaks the pattern: the place word is あそこ, not あこ.

これ それ あれ どれ: things

These stand alone, with no noun after them.

WordExampleMeaning
これこれは わたしの ほんです。This is my book.
それそれは なんですか。What is that (near you)?
あれあれは ふじさんです。That over there is Mt. Fuji.
どれあなたの かさは どれですか。Which one is your umbrella?

この その あの どの: + noun

These can't stand alone. A noun must follow.

WordExampleMeaning
このこの ほんは わたしのです。This book is mine.
そのその かばんは いくらですか。How much is that bag?
あのあの ひとは だれですか。Who is that person?
どのどの バスに のりますか。Which bus do you take?
Say これは ほんです, never このは ほんです. A noun must come straight after この.

ここ そこ あそこ どこ: places

When two people are in the same spot, ここ is where they both are, そこ is a little way off, and あそこ is further away.

WordExampleMeaning
ここここは きょうしつです。This is the classroom.
そこそこに ゆうびんきょくが あります。There's a post office there.
あそこあそこは びょういんです。That place over there is a hospital.
どこえきは どこですか。Where is the station?

こちら そちら あちら どちら: polite

These are the polite versions of the place words. こちら also politely introduces a person, and どちら also means 'which of two'.

WordExampleMeaning
こちらこちらは やまださんです。This is Mr. Yamada.
こちらこちらへ どうぞ。This way, please.
そちらそちらの ちずを みせて ください。Please show me the map on your side.
あちらエレベーターは あちらです。The elevator is over there.
どちらおくには どちらですか。Which country are you from?
Staff in shops and hotels use this row with customers. Use it yourself with people you should be polite to.

こっち そっち あっち どっち: casual

Same meaning as the row above, for friends and family only.

WordExampleMeaning
こっちこっちへ きて。Come over here.
そっちそっちは さむい？Is it cold where you are?
あっちえきは あっちだよ。The station is that way.
どっちどっちが いい？Which one do you want?

こんな そんな あんな どんな: this kind of

These mean 'a thing like this'. A noun must follow.

WordExampleMeaning
こんなこんな かばんが ほしいです。I want a bag like this.
そんなそんな くつは ありません。We don't have shoes like that.
あんなあんな うちに すみたいです。I want to live in a house like that.
どんなどんな ひとですか。What kind of person is he?

The answer flips

What is near me is far from you, so the word changes between question and answer.

QuestionAnswerMeaning
それは なんですか。これは じしょです。What's that? This is a dictionary.
これは なんですか。それは とけいです。What's this? That's a watch.
あれは なんですか。あれは ぎんこうです。What's that over there? That's a bank.
ここは どこですか。ここは とうきょうです。Where are we? This is Tokyo.
A question with あ gets an answer with あ, because the thing is far from both of you.
