はんたいの ことば · Opposites

Words are easier to remember in pairs. Pairs also help with paraphrase questions, where たかくないです can mean the same as やすいです.

あつい has three meanings by sound alone (hot weather, hot to the touch, thick), so its opposite depends on which one you mean.

な-adjectives

WordMeaningOppositeMeaning
すきlikeきらいdislike
じょうずgood atへたbad at

Some opposites cross types. いそがしい and むずかしい and きたない are い-adjectives, but ひま, かんたん and きれい are な-adjectives. びょうき is a noun.
